CHAPTER E3 · BLOCK E EMERGING & REGULATION

手術機器人與 AI 醫療

從 da Vinci 5、Hugo、Mako 到 Aidoc CARE1 基礎模型；從影像 AI 到 PCCP 預定變更控制——本章把 2025–2026 的手術機器人與 AI 醫療地圖一次攤開。

From da Vinci 5, Hugo, and Mako to the Aidoc CARE1 foundation model; from imaging AI to Predetermined Change Control Plans — this chapter unfolds the 2025–2026 map of surgical robotics and AI in medicine.

市場概觀

一、手術機器人市場現況

2025 年全球手術機器人市場規模約 USD 12.93 B，預計 2035 年達 USD 59.55 B（CAGR ~16.5%）。Intuitive Surgical 以 da Vinci 系列穩居超過 70% 市佔，但近三年競爭加劇：Medtronic Hugo RAS、CMR Surgical Versius、Distalmotion Dexter、Asensus Senhance 進入歐亞市場，骨科端 Stryker Mako、Zimmer Biomet ROSA、Medtronic Mazor X 大幅成長。

商業模式：通常為「設備價（USD 1.5–2.5 M）+ 拋棄式器械（EndoWrist 每例 ~USD 1.5–3k）+ 服務年費」三段式營收；intuitive 一年消耗品收入早已超過設備收入，建立強勁的razor-and-blade護城河。

2025 global surgical-robotics market is ~USD 12.93 B, projected to USD 59.55 B by 2035 (CAGR ~16.5%). Intuitive Surgical's da Vinci family holds >70% share, but competition has intensified: Medtronic Hugo RAS, CMR Surgical Versius, Distalmotion Dexter, Asensus Senhance into EU/Asia; orthopedic side dominated by Stryker Mako, Zimmer Biomet ROSA, and Medtronic Mazor X.

Business model is a three-tier "system (USD 1.5–2.5 M) + disposables (EndoWrist instruments ~USD 1.5–3k/case) + service annual fee." Intuitive's consumables revenue has long exceeded system revenue — a classic razor-and-blade moat.

da Vinci 5「力回饋」（Force Feedback）的工程意義：前代 da Vinci 缺乏觸覺，主刀全靠視覺判斷組織張力；da Vinci 5 將每例手術產生的力感測資料量提升 10,000 倍，可量化縫線張力、組織抓握，未來能餵給 AI 模型學習「動作品質」並輔助 telementoring。 Force Feedback in da Vinci 5 — engineering significance: Prior da Vinci had no haptics; surgeons relied on visual cues. da Vinci 5 increases force-sensing data per case by ~10,000×, quantifying suture tension and tissue grip — feeding future AI models on motion quality and enabling telementoring.
影像導引手術

三、影像導引手術

影像導引手術（Image-Guided Surgery, IGS）的核心是「術前影像 + 術中追蹤 + 病人解剖配準」。三大追蹤模態：

  • 光學追蹤——NDI Polaris、Atracsys、Brainlab Curve；紅外攝影機追蹤被動或主動反射球，精度 0.1–0.3 mm，需 line-of-sight。
  • 電磁追蹤——NDI Aurora、Polhemus；磁場下追蹤微線圈感測器，可進體腔（catheter 尖端），但對金屬干擾敏感。
  • 慣性 + 視覺混合——HoloLens 2、Apple Vision Pro、Magic Leap 為基礎的 AR 平台。

AR 手術代表系統：Augmedics XVision（脊椎螺釘導引，2020 FDA 510(k)，將術前 CT 直接疊在手術視野）、Brainlab Mixed Reality（HoloLens 2 + Loop-X 行動 CT）、Medivis SurgicalAR。

Image-Guided Surgery (IGS) hinges on "preop image + intraop tracking + patient anatomy registration." Three tracking modalities:

  • Optical tracking — NDI Polaris, Atracsys, Brainlab Curve; IR cameras track passive/active reflective spheres, 0.1–0.3 mm precision, needs line-of-sight.
  • Electromagnetic tracking — NDI Aurora, Polhemus; magnetic field tracks micro-coil sensors that fit inside catheter tips, but is metal-sensitive.
  • Inertial + visual hybrid — HoloLens 2, Apple Vision Pro, Magic Leap based AR.

AR-surgery flagships: Augmedics XVision (spinal-screw guidance, FDA 510(k) 2020, overlays preop CT directly on the surgical field), Brainlab Mixed Reality (HoloLens 2 + Loop-X mobile CT), Medivis SurgicalAR.

FDA AI/ML 裝置

四、FDA AI/ML 裝置統計

FDA AI/ML-Enabled Medical Device 公開清單為衡量「AI 進入臨床」最直接的指標：截至 2025 年底累計 1,451 件、2025 一年新增 295 件（史上最多）；2026 年 3 月新增 24 件（平均每 31 小時 1 件），4 月新增 27 件。專科分布以放射 76%為主，其次為心血管、神經、眼科。

2025 年具代表性事件：Aidoc CARE1於 2025/2 成為 FDA 首支獲核可的「基礎模型驅動的臨床 AI 系統」（foundation-model-powered clinical AI）；其影像 triage 引擎一次涵蓋顱內出血、肺栓塞、主動脈剝離等多種急症，是「single-tasking → multi-tasking AI 裝置」的轉折點。

FDA's AI/ML-Enabled Medical Device list is the best public proxy for "AI entering the clinic": cumulative 1,451 by end-2025; 2025 alone added 295 (record year); March 2026 added 24 (one every 31 hrs), April 2026 added 27. Radiology 76%; rest cardio, neuro, ophthalmology.

Landmark 2025 event: Aidoc CARE1 (Feb 2025) became the first FDA-cleared foundation-model-powered clinical AI; its imaging triage engine covers intracranial hemorrhage, pulmonary embolism, aortic dissection and more in one model — the pivot from "single-task AI" to "multi-task AI" devices.

代表 AI 裝置

五、代表性 AI 醫療裝置

🧠

Viz.ai (LVO Stroke)

2018 FDA De Novo 核可，首支 AI 中風影像 triage：偵測 CT angiography 中大血管阻塞（LVO）→ 即時透過 App 通知 stroke team；證實縮短 door-to-puncture 時間。後續擴及肺栓塞、主動脈剝離、ICH。

FDA De Novo 2018, the first AI stroke imaging triage: detects large-vessel occlusion (LVO) on CT angio, pings the stroke team via app — proven to cut door-to-puncture time. Later expanded to PE, aortic dissection, ICH.

❤️

HeartFlow FFRct

由冠狀動脈 CT 角影像生成 CFD 模擬 + AI，計算非侵入式 fractional flow reserve（FFR-CT），替代部分侵入式 ICA + FFR 測量。NICE 2017 認可，已納入多國臨床路徑。

Generates a CFD + AI simulation from coronary CTA, producing non-invasive fractional flow reserve (FFR-CT) and avoiding some invasive ICA + FFR. NICE-endorsed 2017; included in multi-country care pathways.

👁️

IDx-DR (Digital Diagnostics)

2018 FDA De Novo，全球首支「自主性 AI 診斷」——基層診所中由非眼科專業人員拍照後，AI 直接出「轉診/未轉診」結果，無需眼科醫師判讀。糖尿病視網膜病變篩檢的里程碑。

FDA De Novo 2018, the world's first autonomous AI diagnosis — primary-care staff capture retinal images, AI outputs "refer / don't refer" without an ophthalmologist. A milestone for diabetic retinopathy screening.

🩺

Caption Guidance

2020 FDA 核可，AI 引導非心臟科醫師取得診斷品質心臟超音波；新冠期間在 ICU 廣泛部署。被 GE Healthcare 2021 收購。

FDA cleared 2020, AI-guided cardiac ultrasound for non-cardiology users — deployed widely in ICUs during COVID. Acquired by GE Healthcare 2021.

🔬

Paige.AI / PathAI

數位病理 AI——Paige Prostate（2021 FDA 首支病理 AI）偵測前列腺癌；PathAI 提供多種癌症分級工具給藥廠與醫院。

Digital pathology AI — Paige Prostate (2021, first FDA pathology AI) detects prostate cancer; PathAI provides cancer-grading tools to pharma and hospitals.

🧪

Tempus / Bayer Calantic / RapidAI

Tempus 結合基因檢測 + RWE + AI 提供腫瘤決策支援；Bayer Calantic影像 AI marketplace；RapidAI 中風/PE/AAA 影像 triage 全套。

Tempus combines NGS + RWE + AI for oncology decision support; Bayer Calantic imaging-AI marketplace; RapidAI end-to-end stroke/PE/AAA imaging triage.

PCCP

七、PCCP 預定變更控制計畫

傳統 510(k) / PMA 模型一上市就「凍結」——任何重大改動須重新送審。但 AI 模型本質會隨資料更新。FDA 自 2019 起提出 Predetermined Change Control Plan (PCCP) 概念，於 2024 年 12 月發布最終指南，正式允許廠商：

  1. 先在原始上市申請中列出「預期變更（modification description）」——例如：增加新影像來源、重新訓練、調整 threshold。
  2. 定義「模型更新流程」——訓練資料、驗證方法、效能下限。
  3. 規範「影響評估」——更新後如何驗證安全、有效、無偏。

遵循已備案 PCCP 的更新不需重新送 510(k)。是 AI/ML SaMD 史上最重要的法規鬆綁；EU MDR 與 IVDR 的對應機制目前透過 2024 年 AI Act + MDCG 指引補位。

Traditional 510(k)/PMA "freezes" a device on launch — any material change triggers a new submission. But AI models inherently update. From 2019 FDA developed the Predetermined Change Control Plan (PCCP); final guidance issued Dec 2024 formally allows sponsors to:

  1. List intended changes (modification description) in the original submission — e.g. new image source, retraining, threshold tweak.
  2. Define the update process — training data, validation method, performance floor.
  3. Specify impact assessment — how safety/effectiveness/no-bias are verified post-update.

Updates inside a filed PCCP do not require a new 510(k). Largest regulatory unlock in AI/ML SaMD history. EU MDR/IVDR equivalents are bridged via the 2024 AI Act + MDCG guidance.

決策樹

八、技術選擇決策樹

🌳 術式 + AI 自主程度決策樹

Q1.術式需要深窄空間 + 高靈巧度 + 高解析縫合（前列腺切除、子宮全切、上消化道）？→ 機器人手術（da Vinci、Hugo、Versius）。Procedure needs deep narrow access + dexterity + fine suturing (prostatectomy, hysterectomy, upper GI)? → Robotic (da Vinci, Hugo, Versius).
Q2.術式為骨科精準切骨/植入定位（膝、髖、肩、脊椎）？→ 骨科導引臂（Mako、ROSA、Mazor X），結合術前 CT 規劃。Procedure is orthopedic precision cutting / placement (knee, hip, shoulder, spine)? → Orthopedic guidance arm (Mako, ROSA, Mazor X) with preop-CT planning.
Q3.術式為標準腹腔鏡可勝任、成本敏感？→ 傳統腹腔鏡 或低價機器人（Dexter、Versius）。Procedure is well-served by standard laparoscopy and cost-sensitive? → Traditional laparoscopy or budget robotic (Dexter, Versius).
Q4.AI 設定為「triage / 提示」，臨床醫師最終決策？→ 常規 510(k)；可考慮 PCCP 提前申報未來更新。AI is "triage / hint", with clinician final-call? → Standard 510(k); consider filing a PCCP for future updates.
Q5.AI 為自主診斷（直接出最終結果無人覆核）？→ De Novo（如 IDx-DR）；風險評估嚴格、需多中心臨床。AI is autonomous diagnosis (final output, no human review)? → De Novo (e.g. IDx-DR); rigorous risk assessment + multicenter trials.
Q6.AI 屬生成式 LLM / 對話 agent？→ 目前多以「臨床支援工具」走 SaMD 路徑；FDA 2025/12 開始討論 GenAI SaMD 專屬指南。AI is a generative LLM / conversational agent? → Mostly filed as a clinical-support SaMD today; FDA began discussing GenAI-specific SaMD guidance in Dec 2025.

程式示例

十、分析 FDA AI/ML 裝置清單

FDA 公開 AI/ML-Enabled Device List CSV。以下程式讀入後依專科分組計數，並輸出前 5 大專科。

FDA publishes the AI/ML-Enabled Device List as CSV. The code reads it, groups by specialty, and prints the top 5.

import pandas as pd

# Download the CSV from FDA's website once and load locally
df = pd.read_csv("fda_ai_ml_devices.csv")

# Standardize column names
df.columns = df.columns.str.strip().str.lower().str.replace(" ", "_")

# Group by specialty/panel and count
top = (df.groupby("lead_panel")
         .size()
         .sort_values(ascending=False)
         .head(5))
print("Top 5 specialties on the FDA AI/ML list:")
print(top)

# Year-on-year additions
df["year"] = pd.to_datetime(df["date_of_final_decision"]).dt.year
print("\nDevices added per year:")
print(df.groupby("year").size())
% Analyze FDA AI/ML device list in MATLAB
T = readtable('fda_ai_ml_devices.csv');

% Standardize column names: lowercase, replace spaces with underscores
T.Properties.VariableNames = ...
   lower(strrep(T.Properties.VariableNames, ' ', '_'));

% Top 5 specialties
[g, names] = findgroups(T.lead_panel);
counts     = accumarray(g, 1);
[~, idx]   = sort(counts, 'descend');
fprintf('Top 5 specialties:\n');
for k = 1:5
    fprintf('  %-20s  %4d\n', names{idx(k)}, counts(idx(k)));
end

% Devices per year
T.year = year(T.date_of_final_decision);
disp(groupsummary(T, 'year'));
