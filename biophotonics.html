CHAPTER D4 · BLOCK D DEVICES & INTERFACES

生醫光電

從 Beer-Lambert 定律到 LASIK、從 OCT 視網膜到 ICG 螢光導引手術、從 STED 超解析到 channelrhodopsin 光遺傳——光是現代生醫工程最強的「無創探針」。

From Beer-Lambert to LASIK, OCT retina to ICG-guided surgery, STED super-resolution to channelrhodopsin optogenetics — light is the most versatile non-invasive probe in modern BME.

光-組織交互

一、光與組織的交互作用

光打到組織會發生四種同時並存的物理事件，其相對強度決定了「能否成像」「能否治療」「能否傷害」：

  1. 吸收（Absorption） — 光子能量被組織分子（水、血紅蛋白、黑色素、膠原蛋白）轉換為熱、化學鍵斷裂或螢光。遵循 Beer-Lambert 定律：
I = I₀ · e−α·x   或   A = ε · c · L   (μa = α 為吸收係數，cm⁻¹)
  1. 散射（Scattering） — 光子方向被組織不均勻性偏折。Rayleigh（粒徑 ≪ λ，∝ 1/λ⁴，例：細胞器微結構）；Mie（粒徑 ~ λ，弱波長相依，例：細胞、粒線體）。散射係數 μs 與 anisotropy g（前向 vs 等向）合稱「reduced scattering coefficient μs' = μs(1−g)」。
  2. 反射（Reflection / Reflectance） — 界面（空氣/角質層、組織/血管）造成 Fresnel 反射。
  3. 螢光（Fluorescence） — 吸收光子後分子激發到 S1，經 vibrational relaxation 後輻射出較長波長光子（Stokes shift，10-50 nm）。是分子成像、ICG 手術導引、PDT 與 FRET 的物理基礎。

組織光學窗（Therapeutic / Optical window，650-1350 nm）：在這個波段，水吸收尚未上升、血紅蛋白吸收已下降、黑色素吸收較弱，光可穿透 1-5 mm 組織。是 NIRS、Pulse oximeter、ICG、photodynamic therapy、optogenetic 神經刺激的「能用」波段。

Light striking tissue undergoes four simultaneous physical processes; their relative strengths decide whether we can image, treat, or harm:

  1. Absorption — photon energy is converted by molecules (water, hemoglobin, melanin, collagen) into heat, bond breakage, or fluorescence. Governed by the Beer-Lambert law:
I = I₀ · e−α·x   or   A = ε · c · L   (μa = α is the absorption coefficient, cm⁻¹)
  1. Scattering — photon direction deflected by tissue inhomogeneities. Rayleigh (particle ≪ λ, ∝ 1/λ⁴, e.g. organelle substructure); Mie (particle ~ λ, weak λ-dependence, e.g. cells, mitochondria). Scattering coefficient μs + anisotropy g combine into the reduced scattering coefficient μs' = μs(1−g).
  2. Reflection / reflectance — Fresnel reflection at boundaries (air/stratum corneum, tissue/vessel wall).
  3. Fluorescence — after photon absorption, molecules excite to S1, vibrationally relax, then emit a longer-λ photon (Stokes shift, 10-50 nm). Physical basis of molecular imaging, ICG surgery, PDT, FRET.

Optical / therapeutic window (650-1350 nm): water absorption has not yet risen, hemoglobin absorption has dropped, melanin absorption is weaker — light penetrates 1-5 mm. The "usable" band for NIRS, pulse oximetry, ICG, PDT, and optogenetic neural stimulation.

💡
核心數字：在 600 nm 處，皮膚 μa ≈ 0.3 cm⁻¹、μs' ≈ 20 cm⁻¹（即「散射主導」）；穿透深度（1/e 衰減）約 1.5-3 mm。在 800 nm（光學窗）處可加深至 5 mm。在 2.9 μm（Er:YAG 雷射，水吸收峰）處吸收係數突增至 ~12000 cm⁻¹，故 Er:YAG 雷射切除 ~1 μm 即停——這是「為什麼 Er:YAG 適合精細表皮」的物理根源。 Key numbers: at 600 nm, skin μa ≈ 0.3 cm⁻¹ and μs' ≈ 20 cm⁻¹ ("scattering-dominant"); penetration (1/e) ~1.5-3 mm. At 800 nm (window) it deepens to ~5 mm. At 2.9 μm (Er:YAG, water absorption peak), μa jumps to ~12,000 cm⁻¹ — so Er:YAG ablates only ~1 μm per pulse, which is physically why Er:YAG suits delicate skin work.

雷射手術

三、雷射手術四典範

👁️

① LASIK 屈光手術

原理：femtosecond laser（IntraLase, 2002 FDA）切出 100 μm 厚 corneal flap，掀開後 excimer 193 nm laser 燒蝕角膜基質（每脈衝 ~0.25 μm，無熱損傷的「光分解 photoablation」），改變角膜曲率矯正屈光不正。今主流為 Femto-LASIK（IntraLase + WaveLight EX500）與 SMILE（small-incision lenticule extraction，只用 femtosecond 不用 excimer）。並發症：dry eye、ectasia、higher-order aberrations。

How it works: a femtosecond laser (IntraLase, FDA 2002) cuts a ~100 μm corneal flap; an excimer laser at 193 nm ablates the underlying stroma (~0.25 μm per pulse — non-thermal photoablation) to reshape corneal curvature and correct refractive error. Today's mainstream: Femto-LASIK (IntraLase + WaveLight EX500) and SMILE (small-incision lenticule extraction; femtosecond only, no excimer). Complications: dry eye, ectasia, higher-order aberrations.

🔥

② 光熱凝固

原理：將組織加熱至 60-100 ℃ 蛋白變性。視網膜光凝（532 nm 或 577 nm）治療糖尿病視網膜病變、視網膜剝離、黃斑水腫；panretinal photocoagulation (PRP) 在周邊視網膜燒出數千點以減少 VEGF 釋放。軟組織止血：CO₂ 雷射切除同時封閉小血管。

Tissue heated to 60-100 °C → protein denaturation. Retinal photocoagulation (532 / 577 nm) treats diabetic retinopathy, retinal detachment, macular edema; panretinal photocoagulation (PRP) places thousands of spots peripherally to reduce VEGF. Soft-tissue hemostasis: CO₂ laser cuts while sealing small vessels.

💊

③ 光動力治療 (PDT)

原理：「光敏劑 (photosensitizer) + 光 + 氧」生成單線態氧 ¹O₂ 與自由基，破壞癌細胞或新生血管。臨床批准光敏劑：

  • Porfimer sodium (Photofrin)：食道癌、Barrett's 食道高度異生（用 630 nm 紅光）。
  • 5-ALA (aminolevulinic acid, Levulan)：皮膚 actinic keratosis；前體式光敏劑（被組織轉化為 PpIX）。
  • Verteporfin (Visudyne)：濕性 AMD（已多被 anti-VEGF 取代）。
  • 5-ALA / Gliolan：膠質瘤螢光導引切除（見下節 fluorescence-guided）。

Photosensitizer + light + oxygen → singlet oxygen ¹O₂ and radicals → destroy tumor cells or neovasculature. Clinically approved sensitizers:

  • Porfimer sodium (Photofrin): esophageal cancer, high-grade Barrett's (630 nm red light).
  • 5-ALA (Levulan): skin actinic keratosis; pro-drug converted to PpIX in tissue.
  • Verteporfin (Visudyne): wet AMD (mostly displaced by anti-VEGF).
  • 5-ALA / Gliolan: fluorescence-guided glioma resection (next section).
💥

④ 光致電離 / 光衝擊

原理：超短脈衝（fs/ps）雷射在組織內聚焦達 ~10¹⁰ W/cm²，產生 plasma → 衝擊波 → 機械切割。應用：YAG capsulotomy（後囊膜混濁）、femtosecond LASIK flap、FLACS 白內障手術（IntraLase iFS、LenSx、Catalys）。優勢：對周邊組織熱影響極小（< 1 μm）。

Ultrashort pulses (fs/ps) focused to ~10¹⁰ W/cm² generate plasma → shockwave → mechanical cleavage. Apps: YAG capsulotomy (posterior capsule opacification), femtosecond LASIK flap, FLACS cataract (IntraLase iFS, LenSx, Catalys). Pro: thermal damage to adjacent tissue is <1 μm.

光學成像

四、光學顯微成像

光學顯微鏡受 Abbe 繞射極限限制：d ≈ λ/(2·NA) ≈ 200 nm（可見光、NA 1.4）。整個現代光學顯微鏡發展史，就是「如何穿透 Abbe 極限」的歷史：

  • 共軛焦顯微鏡（Confocal, Minsky 1955 patent / Zeiss 1980s 商品化）：pinhole 排除焦面外散射光，提升軸向解析力；可做光學切片。
  • 雙光子顯微鏡（Two-photon, Denk & Webb 1990 Cornell）：用 ~800 nm fs laser 同時吸收兩光子（局部高光通量處才有），等效於 ~400 nm 激發，但用近紅外光穿透更深（~ 1 mm 腦皮質）；無焦面外漂白。已成神經科學標配。
  • 光片照明顯微鏡（Light-sheet, SPIM, Huisken 2004）：從側面打出薄片光、垂直方向拍攝；對 zebrafish 胚胎、清明組織、organoid 整體成像極快、漂白少。
  • 超解析（Super-Resolution）：
    • STED（Hell 1994, 2014 Nobel）：用環狀「stimulated emission」光抹掉激發點周邊螢光，解析 30-70 nm。
    • PALM / STORM（Betzig / Hess / Zhuang, 2014 Nobel）：螢光分子隨機開關，定位到 10-30 nm 後重建。
    • MINFLUX（Hell 2017）：結合 STED 環狀光與單分子定位，~1-3 nm 解析（目前光學顯微極限）。

Light microscopy is bounded by Abbe's diffraction limit: d ≈ λ/(2·NA) ≈ 200 nm (visible, NA 1.4). Modern microscopy history is the story of breaking that limit:

  • Confocal (Minsky 1955 patent / Zeiss commercial 1980s): a pinhole rejects out-of-focus scatter, sharpens axial resolution, enables optical sectioning.
  • Two-photon (Denk & Webb 1990 Cornell): simultaneous absorption of two ~800 nm fs photons (only at the focal point's high flux), equivalent to ~400 nm excitation but with NIR penetration (~1 mm in cortex); no out-of-focus bleaching. The neuroscience workhorse.
  • Light-sheet (SPIM, Huisken 2004): illuminate a thin orthogonal sheet, image perpendicular — extremely fast and low-bleach whole-embryo/organoid imaging.
  • Super-resolution:
    • STED (Hell 1994; Nobel 2014): a donut-shaped stimulated-emission beam erases peripheral fluorescence, yielding 30-70 nm.
    • PALM / STORM (Betzig / Hess / Zhuang; Nobel 2014): stochastic on-off blinking lets you localize each molecule to 10-30 nm, then reconstruct.
    • MINFLUX (Hell 2017): combines STED's donut with single-molecule localization — ~1-3 nm resolution (current optical frontier).
OCT

五、OCT 光學同調斷層

OCT（Optical Coherence Tomography）由 Huang, Fujimoto, Puliafito 等於 MIT 1991 提出。原理：用低同調寬頻光源（superluminescent diode, SLD，~50-100 nm bandwidth）+ Michelson 干涉儀；只有「光程差等於 reference arm」的反射光會在偵測器產生干涉訊號——這就決定了「Z 軸解析」能達到 ~ 1-15 μm（取決於 bandwidth）。

OCT 演進：

  • Time-Domain OCT (TD-OCT)：1991-2005 主流，移動 reference mirror 機械掃描；軸向 ~10-15 μm；速度慢（每幀數秒）。
  • Spectral-Domain OCT (SD-OCT)：2003+ 用光譜儀 + 傅立葉轉換；速度提升 50-100 倍（每秒 25,000 A-scans），軸向 ~5-7 μm。
  • Swept-Source OCT (SS-OCT)：1050 nm 可調波長雷射，A-scan rate 100,000-400,000 Hz，深度可達 6 mm（穿透視網膜進入 choroid 與 vitreous）。
  • OCT-Angiography (OCTA)：2014+，用連續多幀的「decorrelation」偵測血流，不需打顯影劑就能成像視網膜微血管網（FAZ、糖尿病視網膜病變）。

臨床應用：

  • 視網膜 OCT（Heidelberg Spectralis、Zeiss Cirrus、Topcon Maestro2、Optos Silverstone）：DR/AMD/glaucoma/macular hole 黃金標準；每年全球做 > 3 千萬次掃描。
  • 心血管 OCT（intravascular, IV-OCT）：Abbott Optis / Dragonfly OPTIS imaging catheter，3 mm 視野、10 μm 解析；冠脈支架 apposition、新生內膜、易碎斑塊評估。
  • 皮膚 OCT：VivoSight Dx 用於基底細胞癌（BCC）邊界判斷。
  • 食道 / 子宮頸 OCT：研究階段 + 部分臨床應用。

OCT was proposed by Huang, Fujimoto, Puliafito at MIT in 1991. Principle: a low-coherence broadband source (superluminescent diode, ~50-100 nm bandwidth) in a Michelson interferometer — only reflections whose path-length matches the reference arm produce interference. That sets Z-axis resolution at ~1-15 μm (bandwidth-dependent).

OCT generations:

  • Time-Domain (TD-OCT): 1991-2005 mainstream; mechanically scanning reference mirror; ~10-15 μm axial; slow (seconds/frame).
  • Spectral-Domain (SD-OCT): 2003+ uses spectrometer + FFT; 50-100× faster (25,000 A-scans/s), ~5-7 μm axial.
  • Swept-Source (SS-OCT): tunable 1050 nm laser, 100,000-400,000 Hz A-scans, 6 mm depth (penetrates retina into choroid/vitreous).
  • OCT-Angiography (OCTA, 2014+): decorrelation between consecutive frames maps blood flow without contrast dye — visualizes retinal capillary plexus (FAZ, diabetic retinopathy).

Clinical apps:

  • Retinal OCT (Heidelberg Spectralis, Zeiss Cirrus, Topcon Maestro2, Optos Silverstone): gold standard for DR/AMD/glaucoma/macular hole; >30 M scans/year globally.
  • Intravascular OCT (IV-OCT): Abbott Optis / Dragonfly OPTIS catheter, 3 mm field, 10 μm resolution; coronary stent apposition, neointima, vulnerable plaque.
  • Skin OCT: VivoSight Dx for basal cell carcinoma margins.
  • Esophageal / cervical OCT: research with some clinical use.
螢光導引

六、螢光導引手術

手術中的螢光成像讓外科醫師「在組織裡看見看不見的東西」：

  • ICG（Indocyanine green）：FDA 1959 核可，激發 ~ 805 nm、發射 ~ 830 nm（NIR）。靜脈注射後 2-3 分鐘進入循環，受白蛋白結合、由肝臟排出（半衰期 3-4 min）。臨床用途：(1) 前哨淋巴結（乳癌、黑色素瘤、子宮頸癌）— Stryker SPY-PHI、Novadaq Pinpoint；(2) 膽道解剖（達文西 Firefly mode 顯示總膽管）；(3) 組織灌流（皮瓣、腸吻合存活性）；(4) 肝臟腫瘤（蓄積於 HCC 與肝細胞）。
  • 5-ALA / Gliolan（5-aminolevulinic acid）：口服前體 → 在膠質瘤細胞內代謝為 PpIX（protoporphyrin IX）→ 405 nm 藍光下發出紅色螢光。2017 FDA 核可用於 high-grade glioma 切除導引；歐洲已使用十餘年。ALA-Glioma Study（Stummer 2006 Lancet Oncol）顯示完整切除率自 36% 提升至 65%、6 個月無進展存活率提升。
  • FITC / GFP / mCherry：研究用螢光蛋白。
  • Tumor-targeted NIR probes（OTL38 pafolacianine, ON-101）：葉酸受體靶向螢光劑；OTL38 2021 FDA 核可用於卵巢癌、2022 核可用於肺癌螢光導引切除。

外科設備整合：Stryker SPY Elite/PHI/Q、Karl Storz NIR/ICG、Olympus VISERA ELITE NIR、Intuitive da Vinci Xi/SP Firefly。

Intraoperative fluorescence lets surgeons "see the invisible in tissue":

  • ICG (indocyanine green): FDA-approved 1959, excitation ~805 nm, emission ~830 nm (NIR). IV-injected, binds albumin, hepatic clearance (t½ 3-4 min). Uses: (1) sentinel lymph node (breast, melanoma, cervical) — Stryker SPY-PHI, Novadaq Pinpoint; (2) biliary anatomy (da Vinci Firefly highlights common bile duct); (3) tissue perfusion (flap, bowel anastomosis viability); (4) liver tumors (HCC accumulation).
  • 5-ALA / Gliolan: oral prodrug → metabolized to PpIX in glioma cells → red fluorescence under 405 nm blue light. FDA-approved 2017 for high-grade glioma resection guidance; widely used in Europe for over a decade. The ALA-Glioma Study (Stummer 2006 Lancet Oncol) raised complete resection from 36% to 65% with improved 6-month PFS.
  • FITC / GFP / mCherry: research fluorescent reporters.
  • Tumor-targeted NIR probes (OTL38 pafolacianine, ON-101): folate-receptor-targeted; OTL38 FDA-approved 2021 (ovarian cancer) and 2022 (lung cancer) for fluorescence-guided resection.

Surgical platforms: Stryker SPY Elite/PHI/Q, Karl Storz NIR/ICG, Olympus VISERA ELITE NIR, Intuitive da Vinci Xi/SP Firefly.

⚠️
螢光導引不是「魔法」：ICG 的特異性來自血管動力學（不是分子靶向），所以「ICG 亮 = 腫瘤」其實只在HCC、blood-rich tumor、灌流活性等特定情境正確。5-ALA 不在低惡性度膠質瘤（low-grade glioma）發出強訊號。Tumor-targeted probes（OTL38）是下一代「分子靶向」螢光劑。 Fluorescence guidance is not magic: ICG specificity stems from vascular dynamics, not molecular targeting — "ICG-bright = tumor" holds only in HCC, blood-rich tumors, and viable perfusion. 5-ALA fluoresces poorly in low-grade gliomas. Tumor-targeted probes (OTL38) represent the next generation of molecularly-targeted fluorescence.
光聲 / 光譜

七、光聲成像與光譜技術

🔊 光聲成像 (PAI)

原理：奈秒級脈衝雷射照射組織 → 局部熱膨脹 → 產生超音波 → 用超音波 transducer 接收。「光的對比 + 超音波的解析與穿透」。對血紅蛋白、黑色素天然有對比，可做 3-7 cm 深度的血氧成像、黑色素瘤 staging。代表系統：iThera MSOT、Seno Imaging Imagio（首支 FDA 核可的 OA/US 乳房成像系統，2021）。

Nanosecond pulsed laser → local thermal expansion → ultrasound emission → US transducer. "Optical contrast + ultrasound resolution & depth." Intrinsic contrast for HbO₂/Hb and melanin enables 3-7 cm-deep oxygenation imaging and melanoma staging. Systems: iThera MSOT, Seno Imaging Imagio (first FDA-cleared OA/US breast imager, 2021).

🌈 拉曼光譜

原理：非彈性散射 — 入射光子失去/獲得分子振動能量 → 出射光譜「指紋」即組成。無標籤、無破壞、分子特異性。SERS（Surface-Enhanced Raman Spectroscopy）以金/銀奈米結構增強 10⁶-10¹⁰。臨床應用：腦腫瘤邊界（Reichmann 2020 Neurosurgery）、stimulated Raman histology（NIO Imaging，神經外科術中組織學）。

Inelastic scattering — incident photons lose/gain vibrational energy → "fingerprint" spectrum. Label-free, non-destructive, molecule-specific. SERS (Surface-Enhanced Raman) enhances 10⁶-10¹⁰ with Au/Ag nanostructures. Clinical: brain tumor margins (Reichmann 2020 Neurosurgery), stimulated Raman histology (NIO Imaging, intraoperative neuropathology).

📡 FTIR 光譜

傅立葉轉換紅外光譜：中紅外（4000-400 cm⁻¹）量分子振動轉動。臨床用於組織分型、生物液體分析、製藥 QC。ATR-FTIR（衰減全反射）可直接量水溶液樣品。

Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy — mid-IR (4000-400 cm⁻¹) probes vibrations/rotations. Used for tissue typing, biofluid analysis, pharma QC. ATR-FTIR (attenuated total reflection) handles aqueous samples directly.

🧠 NIRS 近紅外

用 700-900 nm 光穿透頭骨進入腦皮質（穿透 ~ 1.5 cm），量 oxy-Hb 與 deoxy-Hb 變化估腦氧合與血流。fNIRS（functional NIRS）作為 fMRI 的可攜替代品。腦氧監測儀（Masimo O3、Medtronic INVOS）用於心臟手術 / ICU 監測 cerebral oxygenation。

700-900 nm light penetrates skull into cortex (~1.5 cm depth), tracking oxy-Hb and deoxy-Hb to estimate cerebral oxygenation/flow. fNIRS is a portable alternative to fMRI. Cerebral oximeters (Masimo O3, Medtronic INVOS) monitor cerebral oxygenation in cardiac surgery / ICU.

光遺傳學

八、光遺傳學

光遺傳學是 21 世紀神經科學的革命性技術：把光敏離子通道基因（opsin）植入特定神經元，再用光精準開關它們。三個關鍵分子：

  • Channelrhodopsin-2 (ChR2)：Karl Deisseroth + Ed Boyden + Feng Zhang 2005 Stanford；藍光（470 nm）開啟陽離子通道 → 神經元去極化（excitation）。源自綠藻 Chlamydomonas reinhardtii。
  • Halorhodopsin (NpHR)：黃光（580 nm）開啟氯離子幫浦 → 神經元超極化（inhibition）。源自 Natronomonas pharaonis。
  • Archaerhodopsin (Arch)：綠光開啟質子幫浦 → 強力 hyperpolarization。

臨床轉譯：

  • GenSight Biologics GS030（CNGA2/ChrimsonR + AAV）：視網膜色素變性（retinitis pigmentosa, RP）患者。Sahel et al. Nature Medicine 2021 報告首例患者：注射 AAV 攜帶 ChrimsonR 後，配合特製 goggle，58 歲全盲患者可定位、計數桌上物件——首例光遺傳學人類視覺修復。
  • Bionic Sight BS01 / Nanoscope MCO-010：競爭管線，多中心 phase 2 進行中。
  • RetroSense Therapeutics（後被 Allergan 收購）RST-001：原始首例（2016 IND 啟動）的 RP optogenetic 計畫。

挑戰：(1) 需配戴特製 goggle 把可見光轉成 channelrhodopsin 可激發的高強度單一波長；(2) opsin 對光敏度遠低於人類視紫質，需要 ~ 10⁵× 高的光強度；(3) 只能取代「在 cone 缺失但 bipolar / ganglion cell 仍存活」的患者；(4) 長期 ChrimsonR 表達穩定性仍在追蹤。

Optogenetics is the 21st-century neuroscience revolution: insert light-sensitive ion-channel genes (opsins) into specific neurons, then turn them on/off with light. Three key molecules:

  • Channelrhodopsin-2 (ChR2): Deisseroth + Boyden + Zhang, Stanford 2005; blue light (470 nm) opens cation channel → neuronal depolarization (excitation). From the green alga Chlamydomonas reinhardtii.
  • Halorhodopsin (NpHR): yellow light (580 nm) drives Cl⁻ pump → hyperpolarization (inhibition). From Natronomonas pharaonis.
  • Archaerhodopsin (Arch): green light drives proton pump → strong hyperpolarization.

Clinical translation:

  • GenSight Biologics GS030 (ChrimsonR via AAV): retinitis pigmentosa (RP). Sahel et al. Nature Medicine 2021 reported the first patient — a 58-year-old fully blind patient, after AAV-ChrimsonR injection plus a custom light-stimulating goggle, could locate and count objects on a table. The first optogenetic vision restoration in a human.
  • Bionic Sight BS01 / Nanoscope MCO-010: competing pipelines in multi-center phase 2.
  • RetroSense Therapeutics (acquired by Allergan) RST-001: the original first-in-human RP optogenetic program (IND 2016).

Challenges: (1) requires goggle to convert ambient light into high-intensity narrow-band light an opsin can absorb; (2) opsin photosensitivity is ~10⁵× lower than human rhodopsin, so high intensity is needed; (3) only works when cones are lost but bipolar/ganglion cells survive; (4) long-term ChrimsonR expression stability is still under study.

脈搏血氧

九、脈搏血氧深入解析

脈搏血氧儀是「Beer-Lambert 定律 + ratio-of-ratios」最成功的工程化案例之一。原理回顧（D2 章已建立）：兩波長（660 nm 紅光、940 nm NIR），測動脈搏動 AC / 直流 DC 比，得：

R = (AC660/DC660) / (AC940/DC940)   →   SpO₂ ≈ 110 − 25·R （經驗 fit）

深入細節：

  • Isobestic 波長 ~ 805 nm：HbO₂ 與 Hb 的莫耳吸收係數相等，可用為 reference。NIRS（cerebral oximeter）即用此波長 + 一根 660 nm 或 730 nm。
  • Pulse oximeter 假設只有 HbO₂ + Hb 兩物種。但血液實際上還有：COHb（一氧化碳血紅蛋白）、MetHb（甲基血紅蛋白）、SulfHb。標準兩波長 pulse oximeter 把 COHb 誤判為 HbO₂（兩者在 660 nm 吸收相似），故 CO 中毒患者 SpO₂ 假性正常。
  • CO-oximetry：Masimo Rainbow 系列用 7-12 個波長，可額外量 COHb、MetHb、SpHb（總血紅蛋白）、PVI（plethysmography variability index，灌流變異度）。
  • 2024 FDA 公告：修訂 pulse oximeter accuracy guidance，要求 calibration cohort 含足量深膚色受測者（≥ 25% 受試者為深膚色），以減少 occult hypoxia 偏差。多家廠商（Masimo、Nonin、Medtronic Nellcor）已發布或公告 race-neutral 校正算法。

The pulse oximeter is one of the most successful engineering applications of Beer-Lambert + ratio-of-ratios. Recap (built in D2): two wavelengths (660 nm red, 940 nm NIR) measure arterial AC over DC and yield:

R = (AC660/DC660) / (AC940/DC940)   →   SpO₂ ≈ 110 − 25·R (empirical fit)

Deeper points:

  • Isobestic wavelength ~805 nm: HbO₂ and Hb have equal molar absorptivity, serving as a reference. NIRS cerebral oximeters use this + 660 or 730 nm.
  • Pulse oximeters assume only HbO₂ + Hb. Blood also contains COHb, MetHb, SulfHb. A standard 2-λ device confuses COHb with HbO₂ (similar 660 nm absorption), so SpO₂ reads falsely normal in CO poisoning.
  • CO-oximetry: Masimo Rainbow series uses 7-12 wavelengths, also measuring COHb, MetHb, SpHb (total Hb), PVI (plethysmography variability index).
  • 2024 FDA action: revised pulse oximeter accuracy guidance requires calibration cohorts with sufficient dark-skin representation (≥25% of subjects), reducing occult hypoxia bias. Masimo, Nonin, and Medtronic Nellcor have released or announced race-neutral correction algorithms.

程式示例

十二、Beer-Lambert + SpO₂ 計算

以下實作兩件事：(1) 給定波長吸收係數 α 與組織厚度 x，計算穿透剩餘光強 I = I₀ e^(−αx)；(2) 給定 660 / 940 nm 的 AC/DC，計算 ratio-of-ratios R 與經驗 SpO₂。

Two things below: (1) given α and tissue thickness x, compute I = I₀·e^(−αx); (2) given 660/940 nm AC/DC, compute R and the empirical SpO₂ estimate.

import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

# ===== Part 1: Beer-Lambert tissue penetration =====
x = np.linspace(0, 10, 500)            # depth in mm
alpha = {'532 nm (green)': 3.0,         # /mm, dominated by HbO2
         '660 nm (red)':   0.6,         # window edge
         '940 nm (NIR)':   0.4}        # deep window
I0 = 1.0
plt.figure(figsize=(8,4))
for name,a in alpha.items():
    I = I0 * np.exp(-a*x)
    plt.plot(x, I, label=f'{name}, α={a}/mm')
plt.axhline(1/np.exp(1), color='gray', ls='--', label='1/e')
plt.xlabel('depth (mm)'); plt.ylabel('normalized intensity')
plt.title('Beer-Lambert tissue penetration'); plt.legend(); plt.grid(alpha=.3)

# ===== Part 2: Pulse-ox SpO2 from R-ratio =====
def spo2_from_ratio(ac660, dc660, ac940, dc940):
    R = (ac660/dc660) / (ac940/dc940)
    spo2 = 110 - 25*R                   # empirical, valid 0.4 < R < 3.4
    return R, max(70, min(100, spo2))

# Healthy normoxic example
R,sp = spo2_from_ratio(0.06,1.0, 0.10,1.0)
print(f'Normoxia : R={R:.2f}, SpO2≈{sp:.1f}%')
# Hypoxic example (less pulsatile signal at 940 nm)
R,sp = spo2_from_ratio(0.10,1.0, 0.07,1.0)
print(f'Hypoxia  : R={R:.2f}, SpO2≈{sp:.1f}%')

plt.tight_layout(); plt.show()
% ===== Part 1: Beer-Lambert tissue penetration =====
x = linspace(0, 10, 500);                  % depth (mm)
alpha = [3.0 0.6 0.4];                       % /mm for 532/660/940 nm
names = {'532 nm' '660 nm' '940 nm'};

figure; hold on;
for k = 1:3
    I = I0 * exp(-alpha(k)*x);
    plot(x, I, 'LineWidth', 1.5, 'DisplayName', names{k});
end

% ===== Part 2: SpO2 from R-ratio =====

        (ac660/dc660)/(ac940/dc940), ...
        max(70, min(100, 110 - 25*(ac660/dc660)/(ac940/dc940))));

[R, sp] = spo2(0.06,1.0, 0.10,1.0);
