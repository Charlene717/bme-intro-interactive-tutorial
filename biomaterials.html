CHAPTER A4 · BLOCK A FOUNDATIONS

生醫材料

從鈦合金髖關節到 PEG 水凝膠 BLA、從可吸收支架到 PEEK 3D 列印——本章帶你掌握 4 大材料家族與 ISO 10993 生物相容性測試。

From Ti hip implants to PEG hydrogels, bioresorbable stents to PEEK 3D printing — master the four material families and ISO 10993 biocompatibility testing.

定義

一、什麼是生醫材料？

生醫材料（Biomaterial）是「設計用於與生物系統交互、達成診斷／治療目的的天然或人造材料」（Williams, 1987 並由 ESB 2018 重新校準）。它不是「對人體無害的材料」這麼狹義，而是涵蓋了主動引發生物響應（如骨整合、藥物釋放、組織再生）的所有設計過的材料。

從心血管支架、人工髖關節、骨水泥、隱形眼鏡、人工瓣膜、CGM 感測膜、可植入電極、藥物緩釋微粒、3D 列印氣管支架、到 mRNA 疫苗的脂質奈米粒——這些「東西」的核心都是生醫材料。

A biomaterial is "a natural or synthetic material designed to interact with biological systems for diagnostic or therapeutic purposes" (Williams 1987, recalibrated by ESB 2018). It's not just "harmless to humans" — it covers all engineered materials that actively elicit biological responses (osseointegration, drug release, tissue regen).

Cardiovascular stents, hip implants, bone cement, contact lenses, prosthetic valves, CGM sensor membranes, implantable electrodes, drug-release microparticles, 3D-printed tracheal scaffolds, mRNA-vaccine lipid nanoparticles — at the core of all these "things" are biomaterials.

💡
三大設計目標：(1) 生物相容性（biocompatibility）——身體接受它；(2) 機械適配（mechanical fit）——剛度/強度匹配所替代的組織；(3) 功能性（functionality）——能完成預期的醫療任務（承載、釋放、傳訊、再生）。 Three design goals: (1) Biocompatibility — the body accepts it; (2) Mechanical fit — stiffness/strength matches the replaced tissue; (3) Functionality — performs its medical task (load-bearing, release, signaling, regeneration).

Once implanted, bone "stops bearing load" and atrophies (Wolff's law). Solutions: low-modulus alloys, porous Ti, 3D-printed lattices, composites.

生物相容性

四、ISO 10993 測試矩陣

ISO 10993 系列把所有醫療器材依「接觸性質 × 接觸時間」分類，建議對應的生物相容性測試。三大接觸維度：

  1. 表面接觸（surface）：完整皮膚 / 黏膜 / 受損體表
  2. 外部通訊（external communicating）：血液通路 / 組織骨頭（短期暴露） / 循環血液
  3. 植入（implant）：組織骨頭 / 血液（永久）

接觸時間分 ≤24 h、>24 h~30 d、>30 d。風險越高，測試項目越多。

The ISO 10993 series classifies all medical devices by "contact type × duration" and recommends corresponding biocompatibility tests. Three contact dimensions:

  1. Surface: intact skin / mucosa / breached surface
  2. External communicating: blood path / tissue-bone (short) / circulating blood
  3. Implant: tissue-bone / blood (permanent)

Duration: ≤24 h, >24 h~30 d, >30 d. Higher risk → more tests.

降解與表面

五、可吸收與表面

♻️ 可吸收高分子

PLA（聚乳酸，6–24 月）、PGA（1–4 月）、PLGA（可調 0.5–24 月）、PCL（> 2 年）、PEG（水溶性）。水解切斷酯鍵 → 乳酸/羥乙酸 → CO₂ + H₂O。應用：骨釘、可吸收支架（Abbott Absorb 已退市，新一代 Magmaris 鎂支架）、藥物緩釋。

PLA (6–24 mo), PGA (1–4 mo), PLGA (tunable 0.5–24 mo), PCL (>2 yr), PEG (water-soluble). Hydrolysis cleaves esters → lactic/glycolic acid → CO₂ + H₂O. Apps: bone screws, BVS (Abbott Absorb withdrawn; newer Magmaris Mg), drug release.

🛡️ 表面塗層與改質

骨整合 → HA / TiO₂ 多孔塗層；抗凝血 → heparin / PEG / 抗凝肝素；抗菌 → 銀奈米、antibiotic-loaded、PEG；可降解 → 親水化、cell-attractive 蜜蜂蛋白（RGD）。

Osseointegration: HA/TiO₂ porous coatings. Antithrombogenic: heparin/PEG. Antimicrobial: Ag nanoparticles, antibiotic-loaded. Cell-attractive: RGD peptide.

🌐 水凝膠

含水量 70–99%；天然（明膠、algiate、HA、fibrin、膠原蛋白）或合成（PEG、PHEMA、PNIPAM）。應用：隱形眼鏡（PHEMA）、傷口敷料、藥物緩釋、3D 生物列印 bioink、組織工程 scaffold。

70–99% water; natural (gelatin, alginate, HA, fibrin, collagen) or synthetic (PEG, PHEMA, PNIPAM). Apps: contact lenses (PHEMA), dressings, drug release, bioinks, scaffolds.

🧠 神經友善材料

傳統 Pt-Ir/IrOx 微電極與腦組織 E 差 6 個量級，刺激會引發 gliosis；新一代用 PEDOT-PSS 導電高分子、carbon fiber、graphene、softgel polymer 把模數降下來。Neuralink 用 4 μm 厚的柔性絲。

Conventional Pt-Ir/IrOx microelectrodes are 10⁶× stiffer than brain — provoke gliosis. Next-gen: PEDOT-PSS, carbon fiber, graphene, softgel polymers reduce stiffness. Neuralink uses 4 μm flexible threads.

決策樹

六、材料選擇決策樹

🌳 材料選擇 4 問

Q1.是否需要承載骨頭/牙齒級別的負荷？→ Ti 合金 / CoCr / PEEK；E 接近骨頭，避免 stress shielding。Need bone/dental load-bearing? → Ti alloy / CoCr / PEEK; aim for bone-like E to avoid stress shielding.
Q2.是否與血液直接接觸？→ heparinized PU、ePTFE、PEG 塗層；ISO 10993-4 hemo 必做。Blood contact? → Heparinized PU, ePTFE, PEG coatings; ISO 10993-4 hemo required.
Q3.是否暫時性、希望最終被吸收？→ PLA/PLGA/PCL；調整分子量與比例控制降解速率。Temporary & should resorb? → PLA/PLGA/PCL; tune MW & ratio to control rate.
Q4.是否做為「活組織支架」？→ 水凝膠（明膠/HA/PEG-RGD）+ 細胞 + bioreactor；組織工程章節深入。Living-tissue scaffold? → Hydrogels (gelatin/HA/PEG-RGD) + cells + bioreactor; see TE chapter.

程式示例

八、PLGA 降解曲線

import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

# Simplified bulk hydrolysis: dMW/dt = -k * MW, k depends on LA:GA ratio
t = np.linspace(0,180,200)   # days
ratios = {"50:50 PLGA":0.060, "75:25 PLGA":0.020, "85:15 PLGA":0.011, "100:0 PLA":0.005}

for name,k in ratios.items():
    mw0 = 50000
    mw  = mw0*np.exp(-k*t)
    plt.plot(t, 100*mw/mw0, label=name)

plt.xlabel("Days"); plt.ylabel("Remaining MW (%)")
plt.title("PLGA hydrolysis vs LA:GA ratio")
plt.legend(); plt.grid(alpha=0.3); plt.show()
t  = 0:1:180;
k_vec=[0.060 0.020 0.011 0.005];
labels={'50:50','75:25','85:15','100:0 PLA'};
for i=1:4
  mw=100*exp(-k_vec(i)*t);
  plot(t,mw,'LineWidth',1.5); hold on;
end
xlabel('Days'); ylabel('Remaining MW (%)');
legend(labels); grid on;
