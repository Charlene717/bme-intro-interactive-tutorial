CHAPTER C1 · BLOCK C MECHANICS/TISSUE

生物力學

從骨頭的應力應變、肌腱的黏彈性、到心臟的壓力-容積環與步態分析——本章帶你用工程力學的語彙拆解人體的「結構與運動」。

From bone stress-strain and tendon viscoelasticity to cardiac PV loops and gait analysis — this chapter decodes the human body's structure and motion using engineering mechanics.

基礎力學

一、應力、應變與楊氏模數

應力（stress） σ = F/A，單位 Pa = N/m²（生醫常用 MPa 或 GPa）；應變（strain） ε = ΔL/L₀，無量綱。一維彈性區下 Hooke's law：σ = E · ε，其中 E 為楊氏模數（Young's modulus），代表材料的「剛度」。

典型工程材料的 σ-ε 曲線可分為四階段：(1) 彈性區（elastic）——線性、卸載可恢復；(2) 降伏點（yield, σy）——之後出現永久塑性變形；(3) 極限抗拉強度（UTS, σu）——應力最高點；(4) 斷裂（fracture, σf）。延性（ductile）材料如不鏽鋼 / Ti，斷前有大塑性區與「頸縮」；脆性（brittle）材料如陶瓷 / 皮質骨，幾乎無塑性區、斷面平整。

Stress σ = F/A in Pa = N/m² (BME often uses MPa or GPa); strain ε = ΔL/L₀ (dimensionless). In the elastic regime Hooke's law gives σ = E · ε, where E is the Young's modulus — material stiffness.

A typical σ-ε curve has four phases: (1) elastic — linear, recoverable on unloading; (2) yield point (σy) — permanent plastic deformation begins; (3) ultimate tensile strength (UTS, σu) — peak stress; (4) fracture (σf). Ductile materials (stainless, Ti) show large plastic zone and necking before fracture; brittle materials (ceramics, cortical bone) have almost no plastic region and a flat fracture surface.

💡
三大相關常數：Young's modulus E、shear modulus G、bulk modulus K，與 Poisson's ratio ν 滿足 G = E/2(1+ν)。人體軟組織常假設 ν ≈ 0.49（接近不可壓縮）；骨頭 ν ≈ 0.3。 Three related constants: Young's modulus E, shear G, bulk K — with Poisson's ratio ν satisfying G = E/2(1+ν). Soft tissues ν ≈ 0.49 (nearly incompressible); bone ν ≈ 0.3.
骨骼力學

二、骨骼力學

骨頭是一種分階層複合材料：奈米尺度的羥磷灰石（HA）礦物 + 第一型膠原蛋白；微結構分為皮質骨（cortical / compact）與骨小樑（trabecular / cancellous）。皮質骨密度 ≈ 1.9 g/cm³、孔隙度 5–10%；骨小樑密度 0.2–0.5 g/cm³、孔隙度 50–90%（如蜂巢）。

  • 皮質骨：E ≈ 17 GPa（長軸方向）、σUTS ≈ 130 MPa 拉伸 / 200 MPa 壓縮。
  • 骨小樑：E ≈ 0.1–0.5 GPa、σUTS ≈ 1–10 MPa，對方向敏感。
  • 異向性（anisotropy）：沿哈氏管軸向強約 2–3 倍橫向。
  • Wolff's law（1892）：骨頭會依長期應力場重塑——多承重的區域變厚、少承重萎縮。Stress shielding 即由此而來。

Bone is a hierarchical composite: nanoscale hydroxyapatite (HA) mineral + type-I collagen. Two micro-structures: cortical (compact) and trabecular (cancellous). Cortical density ≈ 1.9 g/cm³, porosity 5–10%; trabecular 0.2–0.5 g/cm³, porosity 50–90% (honeycomb-like).

  • Cortical bone: E ≈ 17 GPa (long axis), σUTS ≈ 130 MPa tensile / 200 MPa compressive.
  • Trabecular bone: E ≈ 0.1–0.5 GPa, σUTS ≈ 1–10 MPa; highly direction-dependent.
  • Anisotropy: ~2-3× stronger along the Haversian (osteonal) axis than transverse.
  • Wolff's law (1892): Bone remodels in response to long-term stress fields — load-bearing regions thicken, unloaded regions atrophy. Stress shielding follows directly from this.
⚠️
骨質疏鬆與骨折風險：停經後女性 BMD 每年下降約 1–3%；當 T-score ≤ −2.5（骨質疏鬆），髖部骨折風險指數型上升。臨床評估工具：DXA + FRAX 10 年骨折機率；治療：bisphosphonates、denosumab、teriparatide、romosozumab。 Osteoporosis & fracture risk: Postmenopausal BMD drops 1–3%/yr; T-score ≤ −2.5 = osteoporosis, hip-fracture risk rises exponentially. Clinical tools: DXA + FRAX 10-yr fracture probability. Drugs: bisphosphonates, denosumab, teriparatide, romosozumab.
軟組織

三、軟組織黏彈性

軟組織（肌腱、韌帶、皮膚、血管、心肌）不是純彈性的——它們時間相依：相同應變下、加載速率越快應力越大。三大現象：

  • 應力鬆弛（stress relaxation）：固定 ε，σ(t) 隨時間下降。
  • 潛變（creep）：固定 σ，ε(t) 隨時間增加。
  • 遲滯（hysteresis）：加載與卸載曲線形成迴圈，迴圈面積 = 該循環消散的能量。

常用三種簡化黏彈模型：

  1. Maxwell 模型（彈簧 + 阻尼器串聯）：dε/dt = (1/E) dσ/dt + σ/η；擅長描述「應力鬆弛」（指數衰減）。
  2. Kelvin-Voigt 模型（並聯）：σ(t) = E·ε(t) + η·dε/dt；擅長描述「潛變」（指數逼近）。
  3. 標準線性固體 SLS（Zener）：Maxwell 並聯一彈簧；可同時擬合鬆弛與潛變，是肌腱、皮膚常用模型。

膠原纖維招募（fiber recruitment）：肌腱/韌帶 σ-ε 曲線有 toe region（小應變、彎曲的膠原逐步被拉直）→ linear region（所有纖維對齊承載）→ failure。Toe 區是非線性的關鍵特徵。

Soft tissues (tendons, ligaments, skin, vessels, myocardium) are not purely elastic — they are time-dependent: faster loading → higher stress at the same strain. Three hallmarks:

  • Stress relaxation: at fixed ε, σ(t) decays over time.
  • Creep: at fixed σ, ε(t) grows over time.
  • Hysteresis: loading and unloading form a loop; loop area = energy dissipated per cycle.

Three common simplified viscoelastic models:

  1. Maxwell (spring + dashpot in series): dε/dt = (1/E) dσ/dt + σ/η; captures stress relaxation (exponential).
  2. Kelvin-Voigt (parallel): σ(t) = E·ε(t) + η·dε/dt; captures creep (exponential approach).
  3. Standard linear solid (SLS, Zener): Maxwell unit in parallel with a spring; fits both relaxation and creep — workhorse for tendon and skin.

Collagen fiber recruitment: Tendon/ligament σ-ε exhibits a toe region (low strain, crimped fibers progressively straighten) → linear region (all aligned, bearing load) → failure. The toe region is the signature nonlinearity.

心臟力學

四、心臟力學

❤️

PV 環四階段

(1) 等容收縮：mitral 關、aortic 未開，壓力升、容積不變；(2) 射血：aortic 開，容積由 EDV → ESV；(3) 等容鬆弛：aortic 關、mitral 未開；(4) 充填：mitral 開，容積由 ESV → EDV。Stroke volume SV = EDV − ESV ≈ 70 mL。

(1) Isovolumic contraction: mitral closed, aortic shut, pressure ↑ at constant V; (2) Ejection: aortic opens, V drops EDV → ESV; (3) Isovolumic relaxation; (4) Filling: mitral opens, V grows ESV → EDV. Stroke volume SV = EDV − ESV ≈ 70 mL.

📈

Frank-Starling 機制

EDV 增加（前負荷 preload 上升）→ 心肌纖維拉長 → 收縮力增、SV 增加。是心臟自我調節輸出的內在機制；在 ESV-pressure 曲線上斜率 = 收縮性指標 Emax（end-systolic elastance）。

↑EDV (preload) → fibers lengthen → stronger contraction → ↑SV. Intrinsic auto-regulation. On the ESP–V relation, slope = Emax (end-systolic elastance) — the standard contractility index.

🩺

後負荷與舒張

後負荷 ≈ 主動脈壓 / SVR；高血壓 → 後負荷上升、SV 下降、肥厚（HCM）。舒張功能不全（HFpEF）：EF 正常但 EDPVR（end-diastolic pressure-volume relation）斜率變陡——「心室太硬」、充填受限。

Afterload ≈ aortic pressure / SVR; hypertension → ↑afterload, ↓SV, hypertrophy (HCM). HFpEF (diastolic dysfunction): EF normal but EDPVR slope steepens — stiff ventricle, impaired filling.

⚙️

臨床心輸出量

CO = SV × HR ≈ 5 L/min（靜息）；運動可達 25 L/min。臨床測量：Swan-Ganz（熱稀釋）、Fick 原理（VO₂ / [A-V O₂ 差]）、echocardiography Doppler、cardiac MRI（黃金標準）。

CO = SV × HR ≈ 5 L/min at rest; up to 25 L/min in exercise. Clinical measurement: Swan-Ganz (thermodilution), Fick principle (VO₂ / [A-V O₂ diff]), echo Doppler, cardiac MRI (gold standard).

步態分析

五、步態分析

一個正常步態週期分 站立期（stance, ~60%）與 擺盪期（swing, ~40%）。站立期可細分：腳跟著地 → 足平放 → 中站 → 腳跟離地 → 腳趾離地。雙腳同時著地（double support）約 10–12%，跑步時為 0。

關鍵量測：

  • Ground Reaction Force（GRF）：三軸測力板（force plate, Kistler / AMTI / Bertec），垂直分量呈現雙駝峰（heel-strike 峰 ≈ 1.0–1.2 BW、push-off 峰 ≈ 1.1 BW）。
  • 動作捕捉（mocap）：Vicon、OptiTrack 紅外線反射標記 + 高速攝影機；新一代以 markerless（Theia 3D、Captury）+ 深度學習 2D-pose（OpenPose、MediaPipe → biomechanical-grade DeepLabCut）。
  • 表面 EMG（sEMG）：Delsys、Noraxon；測肌肉激活時序與強度。
  • 肌肉骨骼模型：OpenSim（Stanford，開源）、AnyBody Technology（商業）；輸入 mocap + GRF → 反向動力學 + 反向動力學 + 靜態最佳化 → 估算肌肉力、關節接觸力。

臨床應用：腦性麻痺手術術前規劃、義肢設計、跑步傷害分析（patellofemoral pain、ITB syndrome）、人工關節術後評估。

One gait cycle = stance (~60%) + swing (~40%). Stance subdivides into heel-strike → foot-flat → mid-stance → heel-off → toe-off. Double support ≈ 10–12% in walking, 0% in running.

Key measurements:

  • Ground reaction force (GRF): tri-axial force plates (Kistler / AMTI / Bertec); vertical component shows a double bump (heel-strike peak ≈ 1.0–1.2 BW, push-off ≈ 1.1 BW).
  • Motion capture: Vicon, OptiTrack (IR-reflective markers + high-speed cameras); newer markerless systems (Theia 3D, Captury) + deep-learning 2D pose (OpenPose, MediaPipe → DeepLabCut biomechanics-grade).
  • Surface EMG (sEMG): Delsys, Noraxon; muscle activation timing & intensity.
  • Musculoskeletal models: OpenSim (Stanford, open source), AnyBody (commercial); inputs = mocap + GRF → inverse kinematics + inverse dynamics + static optimization → muscle & joint-contact forces.

Clinical applications: cerebral-palsy surgical planning, prosthetic design, running-injury analysis (patellofemoral pain, ITB syndrome), post-arthroplasty outcomes.

程式示例

九、程式示例

(a) 簡支樑中點彎曲應力 σ = M·y/I（用於估算骨頭、植入物中段最大應力）。(b) Kelvin-Voigt 模型對階躍應力的潛變響應 ε(t) = (σ₀/E)·(1−e−Et/η)。

(a) Simply-supported beam mid-span bending stress σ = M·y/I (rough estimate for bone/implant mid-shaft). (b) Kelvin-Voigt creep response to a step stress: ε(t) = (σ₀/E)·(1−e−Et/η).

import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
from scipy.integrate import odeint

# (a) Beam bending — long bone mid-shaft
F    = 700           # body weight (N)
L    = 0.40          # femur length (m)
d_o  = 0.028; d_i = 0.016   # outer/inner diameter (m)
I    = np.pi*(d_o**4 - d_i**4)/64
M    = F*L/4          # simply supported, mid-point load
y    = d_o/2
sigma= M*y/I
print(f"Max bending stress = {sigma/1e6:.1f} MPa")

# (b) Kelvin-Voigt creep
E, eta, sigma0 = 1e6, 5e6, 1e4   # Pa, Pa.s, Pa
def dedt(eps,t): return (sigma0 - E*eps)/eta
t = np.linspace(0,30,300)
eps = odeint(dedt,0,t)
plt.plot(t,eps); plt.xlabel("t (s)"); plt.ylabel("strain")
plt.title("Kelvin-Voigt creep under step stress"); plt.grid(alpha=.3); plt.show()
% (a) Beam bending stress
F = 700; L = 0.40; d_o = 0.028; d_i = 0.016;
I = pi*(d_o^4-d_i^4)/64; M = F*L/4; y = d_o/2;
sigma = M*y/I;
fprintf('Bending stress = %.1f MPa\n', sigma/1e6);

% (b) Kelvin-Voigt creep with ode45
E = 1e6; eta = 5e6; s0 = 1e4;
[t,eps] = ode45(@(t,e)(s0-E*e)/eta,[0 30],0);
plot(t,eps,'LineWidth',1.5); grid on;
xlabel('t (s)'); ylabel('strain');
title('Kelvin-Voigt creep');

十、關鍵術語

Young's modulusE = σ/ε，材料的剛度，單位 Pa。骨頭 17 GPa、Ti 110 GPa。   Poisson's ratioν = −ε橫/ε軸；軟組織 ~0.49，骨 ~0.3。   Anisotropy材料性質與方向有關；皮質骨沿骨長軸最強。   Wolff's law骨頭依長期應力場重塑；應力大區增厚、應力小區萎縮。   Viscoelasticity兼具彈性與黏性；表現為 stress relaxation、creep、hysteresis。   SLSStandard Linear Solid (Zener model)；Maxwell + 並聯彈簧，工程肌腱常用。   PV loop壓力-容積環；四階段；曲線下面積 = stroke work。   Frank-StarlingEDV ↑ → 收縮力 ↑ → SV ↑；心臟自我調節輸出。   EmaxEnd-systolic elastance；ESP-V 線斜率；標準收縮性指標。   GRFGround Reaction Force；地面對腳的反作用力，三軸測力板測量。   OpenSimStanford 開源肌肉骨骼模擬平台；mocap + GRF 反推肌力。   FEAFinite Element Analysis；把連續體切成小元素求解 PDE。
