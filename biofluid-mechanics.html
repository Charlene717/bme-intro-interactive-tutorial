CHAPTER C2 · BLOCK C MECHANICS/TISSUE

生物流體力學與循環

血液的非牛頓特性、Poiseuille 與 Womersley 流動、人工瓣膜與 LVAD、心血管 CFD——本章把工程流體力學帶進循環系統。

Non-Newtonian blood, Poiseuille and Womersley flow, prosthetic valves and LVADs, cardiovascular CFD — engineering fluid mechanics for the circulation.

血液流變

一、血液流變

血液是懸浮液：~55% 血漿（牛頓流體，μ ≈ 1.2 cP）+ 45% 細胞（紅血球 RBC、白血球、血小板）。整體呈非牛頓行為——剪切變稀（shear-thinning）：低剪切率時 RBC 聚集成「rouleaux」黏度高（> 10 cP），高剪切率（> 100 s⁻¹）RBC 變形排列、黏度降至 3–4 cP。

常用模型：

  • Newtonian 近似：大血管（aorta, large arteries），D > 1 mm、剪切率高，μ ≈ 3.5 cP。
  • Casson 模型：√τ = √τy + √(μ·γ̇)；含 yield stress τy，適用於低剪切（小靜脈、stagnation 區）。
  • Carreau-Yasuda：四參數模型，覆蓋整段剪切範圍，是 CFD 中血液最常用模型。

血容比（hematocrit, Hct）：男 40–54%、女 36–48%。Hct ↑ → 黏度指數型上升（polycythemia, doping → 中風風險）。Fåhræus-Lindqvist 效應：管徑 < 0.3 mm 時表觀黏度下降——RBC 沿軸心流動。

Blood is a suspension: ~55% plasma (Newtonian, μ ≈ 1.2 cP) + 45% cells (RBCs, WBCs, platelets). Globally non-Newtonian, shear-thinning: at low shear, RBCs aggregate into rouleaux (μ > 10 cP); at high shear (γ̇ > 100 s⁻¹), RBCs deform and align, dropping μ to 3–4 cP.

Common models:

  • Newtonian approximation: large vessels (aorta, large arteries), D > 1 mm, high shear; μ ≈ 3.5 cP.
  • Casson: √τ = √τy + √(μ·γ̇); yield-stress fluid, good for low-shear regions (venules, stagnation).
  • Carreau-Yasuda: 4-parameter, full shear range; the workhorse for CFD.

Hematocrit (Hct): ♂ 40–54%, ♀ 36–48%. ↑Hct → viscosity rises exponentially (polycythemia, blood doping → stroke risk). Fåhræus-Lindqvist effect: in vessels < 0.3 mm apparent viscosity falls — RBCs cluster on the axis.

💡
記憶要點：主動脈、大動脈用 Newtonian 即可；想模擬 stent strut 周邊回流、靜脈瓣、aneurysm 內 stagnation，必須用非牛頓模型（Casson / Carreau-Yasuda）。 Rule of thumb: Newtonian is fine for aorta & large arteries; for stent-strut recirculation, venous valves, or aneurysm stagnation, you need non-Newtonian (Casson / Carreau-Yasuda).
流動方程

二、基本流動方程

1. Poiseuille flow（穩態層流圓管）：

Q = π · ΔP · r⁴ / (8μL)

流量對半徑 4 次方敏感——半徑減一半，流量降 16 倍（解釋為什麼血管狹窄如此危險、為什麼小兒科氣管插管要小心）。

2. Reynolds number：

Re = ρ · v · D / μ

直管中 Re < 2100 為層流（laminar）、2100–4000 為過渡、> 4000 為紊流（turbulent）。主動脈 Re ≈ 4000，但因為脈動（pulsatile）且管短，實際只在收縮期峰值短暫紊流；多數時間仍可視為層流或過渡。

3. Womersley number（脈動流參數）：

α = R · √(ωρ/μ)

其中 ω = 2π·HR (rad/s) ≈ 7.5 rad/s。α < 1 時脈動效應小、速度剖面近似拋物線（Poiseuille）；α > 10 時慣性主導、剖面變「方塊狀」、流速與壓力梯度有相位差（aorta α ≈ 10–15、femoral α ≈ 4、capillary α ≪ 1）。Womersley 解用 Bessel 函數 J0。

1. Poiseuille flow (steady laminar pipe):

Q = π · ΔP · r⁴ / (8μL)

Flow scales with the 4th power of radius — halving the radius cuts flow 16-fold (why arterial stenosis is so dangerous, why pediatric airway intubation needs care).

2. Reynolds number:

Re = ρ · v · D / μ

Straight pipe: Re < 2100 laminar, 2100–4000 transitional, > 4000 turbulent. Aortic Re ≈ 4000, but flow is pulsatile and the pipe is short, so genuine turbulence appears only briefly at peak systole.

3. Womersley number (pulsatile parameter):

α = R · √(ωρ/μ)

ω = 2π·HR (rad/s) ≈ 7.5 rad/s. α < 1: pulsatile effects small, parabolic Poiseuille-like profile; α > 10: inertia dominates, profile flattens (blunt), velocity lags pressure (aorta α ≈ 10–15, femoral α ≈ 4, capillary α ≪ 1). Womersley solution uses Bessel function J0.

脈波

三、波形與脈波速度

動脈壓力波由收縮期外推 + 從遠端反射回波疊加而成。年輕健康者反射波回到時近舒張期、抬升 diastolic pressure（augmentation index 低）；硬化動脈反射波太快回到、堆疊在收縮峰上、收縮壓上升（augmentation index 高，是動脈硬化指標）。

Moens-Korteweg 方程（脈波速度 PWV）：

PWV = √(E · h / (2ρ · r))

E = 血管壁彈性模數、h = 壁厚、r = 半徑。年輕健康主動脈 PWV ≈ 5 m/s；70 歲動脈硬化者 PWV ≈ 12 m/s。臨床用 carotid-femoral PWV（cf-PWV）作為心血管事件預測指標——> 10 m/s 視為高風險。

波形成分：(1) 主收縮波 → (2) 切跡（dicrotic notch，aortic valve 關閉）→ (3) 舒張回波。Windkessel 模型用 RC 等效電路解釋 diastolic decay。

The arterial pressure wave = forward systolic wave + reflected wave from periphery. In healthy young vessels, the reflection arrives in diastole, augmenting diastolic pressure (low augmentation index). In stiff aged arteries, the reflection arrives early and piles onto systolic peak (high augmentation index — a stiffness marker).

Moens-Korteweg (pulse wave velocity):

PWV = √(E · h / (2ρ · r))

E = wall modulus, h = wall thickness, r = radius. Healthy young aorta PWV ≈ 5 m/s; stiff 70-yr-old PWV ≈ 12 m/s. Clinically, carotid-femoral PWV (cf-PWV) > 10 m/s is high cardiovascular risk.

Waveform features: (1) main systolic peak → (2) dicrotic notch (aortic valve closure) → (3) diastolic reflection. The Windkessel model uses an RC circuit analogue to explain diastolic decay.

心血管裝置

四、心血管裝置

💗

人工瓣膜

(1) 機械瓣（mechanical）：St. Jude Medical bileaflet（pyrolytic carbon），耐久 > 25 年，但需終生 warfarin 抗凝。(2) 生物瓣（bioprosthetic）：porcine 或 bovine pericardium，無需長期抗凝、10–15 年退化。(3) TAVR（經導管）：Edwards SAPIEN 3 Ultra、Medtronic Evolut FX；2019 起獲 FDA 核准用於低風險病人，已取代傳統開胸換瓣為主流。

(1) Mechanical: St. Jude bileaflet (pyrolytic carbon); > 25-yr durability but lifelong warfarin. (2) Bioprosthetic: porcine/bovine pericardium; no chronic anticoagulation, 10–15 yr degeneration. (3) TAVR: Edwards SAPIEN 3 Ultra, Medtronic Evolut FX; FDA-cleared for low-risk since 2019, now dominant over open-heart valve replacement.

🔄

LVAD

HeartMate 3（Abbott）為 2025 年市場主導，採用全磁懸浮 centrifugal pump、人工脈動（intrinsic pulse），降低 hemolysis 與血栓。流量 ≈ 5–6 L/min，植入後 5 年存活率 ~ 60%。臨床路徑：bridge-to-transplant、destination therapy。下一代 BiVACOR TAH 2024 進入 FDA early feasibility study。

HeartMate 3 (Abbott) leads 2025 market — fully maglev centrifugal pump with intrinsic pulse mode, lowering hemolysis and thrombus. ~ 5–6 L/min, 5-yr survival ~ 60%. Bridge-to-transplant or destination therapy. Next-gen BiVACOR TAH entered FDA early feasibility 2024.

🫁

ECMO

VV-ECMO（venovenous）：嚴重呼衰，從中央靜脈引血 → oxygenator → 還血至靜脈；不取代心臟。
VA-ECMO（venoarterial）：心源性休克，引血 → 還血至動脈，繞過心肺；增加後負荷，常需 IABP / Impella 卸壓。COVID-19 期間 ECMO 使用激增。

VV-ECMO: severe respiratory failure — central vein → oxygenator → vein; heart untouched.
VA-ECMO: cardiogenic shock — vein → artery, bypassing heart-lungs; raises afterload, often needs IABP / Impella unloading. COVID-19 caused a major surge.

💧

血液透析

對流（convection）+ 擴散（diffusion）跨半透膜清除尿素、肌酸酐；典型流量 QB ≈ 300 mL/min、QD 500 mL/min。clearance K = QB·(Cin−Cout)/Cin。常見膜：聚醚碸 PES、聚碸 PSU、cellulose triacetate。連續腎臟替代療法（CRRT）用於 ICU。

Convection + diffusion across a semi-permeable membrane to clear urea, creatinine; typical QB ≈ 300 mL/min, QD 500 mL/min. Clearance K = QB·(Cin−Cout)/Cin. Common membranes: PES, PSU, cellulose triacetate. CRRT for ICU.

🩸

冠脈支架

金屬骨架（CoCr、Pt-Cr）+ 藥物塗層（everolimus, zotarolimus）+ 可吸收 polymer。流體議題：strut 後方的低速回流區（low WSS, oscillatory shear）促 neointimal hyperplasia 與再狹窄；CFD 用來優化 strut 厚度（< 80 μm）、覆蓋率、cell 設計。

Metal scaffold (CoCr, Pt-Cr) + drug coating (everolimus, zotarolimus) + bioresorbable polymer. Fluidic concerns: low-WSS / oscillatory zone behind struts drives neointimal hyperplasia & restenosis. CFD optimizes strut thickness (< 80 μm), coverage, cell design.

🧪

微流體

微通道（10–500 μm）內 Re ≪ 1（蠕流）；擴散主導混合（被動 mixer 用 herringbone 結構）。應用：POC 診斷（COVID 抗原卡）、CTC 分離、organ-on-chip、droplet-based scRNA-seq（10x Genomics）。

Microchannels (10–500 μm) operate at Re ≪ 1 (Stokes flow); diffusion-dominated mixing (passive mixers use herringbone grooves). Apps: POC diagnostics (COVID antigen), CTC sorting, organ-on-chip, droplet scRNA-seq (10x Genomics).

CFD 應用

五、心血管 CFD

計算流體力學（CFD）解 Navier-Stokes 方程：

ρ(∂v/∂t + v·∇v) = −∇p + μ∇²v + f

BME 常用軟體：ANSYS Fluent / CFX、OpenFOAM（開源）、Star-CCM+、COMSOL、SimVascular（開源，心血管專用 patient-specific pipeline：影像分割 → 表面建模 → 網格 → CFD → WSS map）。

關鍵輸出 — 牆面剪應力（WSS）：

  • 正常 WSS：1–7 Pa（10–70 dyn/cm²）
  • 低 WSS（< 0.4 Pa）+ 振盪（OSI > 0.2）→ 內皮活化、發炎、動脈粥樣硬化（atherosclerosis）初發部位（頸動脈分叉、冠脈彎曲處）
  • 高 WSS（> 10 Pa）→ aneurysm 成長與破裂風險上升（cerebral aneurysm）

流-結構耦合（FSI）：瓣膜 leaflet 動態、TAVR 部署、aneurysm 壁變形——必須把血流與血管壁變形耦合解，常用 ALE（Arbitrary Lagrangian-Eulerian）或 IBM（Immersed Boundary Method）。

CFD solves Navier-Stokes:

ρ(∂v/∂t + v·∇v) = −∇p + μ∇²v + f

Common software: ANSYS Fluent/CFX, OpenFOAM (open source), Star-CCM+, COMSOL, and SimVascular (open source, patient-specific cardiovascular pipeline: image segmentation → surface model → mesh → CFD → WSS map).

Key output — Wall Shear Stress (WSS):

  • Normal WSS: 1–7 Pa (10–70 dyn/cm²)
  • Low WSS (< 0.4 Pa) + oscillatory (OSI > 0.2) → endothelial activation, inflammation, atherosclerosis initiation (carotid bifurcation, coronary bends)
  • High WSS (> 10 Pa) → aneurysm growth and rupture risk (cerebral aneurysms)

Fluid-Structure Interaction (FSI): valve leaflet dynamics, TAVR deployment, aneurysm wall deformation — must couple blood flow with vessel wall deformation; use ALE (Arbitrary Lagrangian-Eulerian) or IBM (Immersed Boundary Method).

七、流動分析決策樹

🌳 流動分析方法

Q1.流動是穩態？管徑均勻？非脈動？→ Poiseuille（解析解）；只算 Q, τ。Steady, uniform pipe, non-pulsatile? → Poiseuille (analytical); compute Q, τ.
Q2.脈動但直管？α 重要嗎？→ Womersley 解析解（Bessel J0）；正弦壓力梯度。Pulsatile, straight pipe? Need α? → Womersley (Bessel J0) for sinusoidal pressure gradient.
Q3.幾何複雜（分叉、彎曲、aneurysm）？→ CFD（ANSYS / OpenFOAM / SimVascular）；3D Navier-Stokes。Complex geometry (bifurcation, bend, aneurysm)? → CFD (ANSYS / OpenFOAM / SimVascular); 3D NS.
Q4.血管壁要變形 / 瓣膜要拍動？→ FSI（ALE 或 IBM 法）；流-結構耦合。Wall deforms / valve flaps? → FSI (ALE or IBM); fluid-structure coupling.
Q5.微通道 / 細胞排序？→ Stokes 流（Re ≪ 1），常用解析或 COMSOL；考慮擴散與電動學。Microchannel / cell sorting? → Stokes flow (Re ≪ 1), analytical or COMSOL; add diffusion + electrokinetics.
程式示例

八、Womersley 程式

解析解：直圓管在正弦壓力梯度下的速度剖面 u(r,t)，使用 Bessel function of complex argument J0。

Analytical solution for sinusoidal pressure gradient in a straight circular pipe; uses Bessel function J0 of complex argument.

import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
from scipy.special import jv

# Aortic-like pulsatile flow
R     = 0.012           # radius (m)
mu    = 0.0035          # Pa.s
rho   = 1060            # kg/m^3
HR    = 75              # bpm
omega = 2*np.pi*HR/60
alpha = R*np.sqrt(omega*rho/mu)
print(f"Womersley alpha = {alpha:.2f}")

K  = 2000             # pressure-gradient amplitude (Pa/m)
i  = 1j
r  = np.linspace(0,R,80)
t  = np.linspace(0, 2*np.pi/omega, 8)
fig,ax = plt.subplots(figsize=(7,4))
for tk in t:
    arg = (i**1.5)*alpha*r/R
    argR= (i**1.5)*alpha
    u   = (K/(i*omega*rho))*(1 - jv(0,arg)/jv(0,argR))*np.exp(i*omega*tk)
    ax.plot(r*1000, u.real, label=f"t={tk*1000:.0f} ms")
ax.set_xlabel("r (mm)"); ax.set_ylabel("u (m/s)")
ax.set_title(f"Womersley profile, alpha={alpha:.1f}"); ax.legend(); plt.show()
R = 0.012; mu = 0.0035; rho = 1060;
HR = 75; w = 2*pi*HR/60;
alpha = R*sqrt(w*rho/mu);
fprintf('alpha = %.2f\n', alpha);

K = 2000;  ii = 1i;
r = linspace(0,R,80);
t = linspace(0, 2*pi/w, 8);
for k = 1:length(t)
  arg  = (ii^1.5)*alpha*r/R;
  argR = (ii^1.5)*alpha;
  u = (K/(ii*w*rho)).*(1 - besselj(0,arg)./besselj(0,argR)).*exp(ii*w*t(k));
  plot(r*1000, real(u),'LineWidth',1.3); hold on;
end
xlabel('r (mm)'); ylabel('u (m/s)');
title(sprintf('Womersley alpha=%.1f',alpha)); grid on;
