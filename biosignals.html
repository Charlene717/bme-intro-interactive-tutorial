CHAPTER B1 · BLOCK B SIGNALS & IMAGES

生醫訊號：身體的電報

ECG、EEG、EMG、PPG、SpO₂、血壓——六種主流生醫訊號的物理來源、振幅、頻率、取樣率、電極與雜訊全景圖。

ECG, EEG, EMG, PPG, SpO₂, BP — the physical origin, amplitude, frequency, sampling, electrodes, and noise sources of six core biosignals.

定義

一、什麼是生醫訊號？

生醫訊號（biosignal）泛指可從生物體上量測到、且隨時間變化的物理量，包括電位（mV、μV）、壓力（mmHg）、聲學（dB）、光學（透光率）、化學濃度（mmol/L）與機械量（力、位移）。在臨床與工程實務中，最常被連續記錄、分析、診斷的是「電生理訊號」與「血流／呼吸生理訊號」兩大類。

BME 工程師對任一個生醫訊號，第一個要問的三個問題是：(1) 它從哪來？（細胞 / 組織 / 系統層次的生成機制）；(2) 它有多大？多快？（典型振幅、頻寬、所需取樣率）；(3) 它會被誰污染？（雜訊與假影來源、共模、漂移）。本章建立這個全景表。

A biosignal is any time-varying physical quantity measured from a living organism — electrical potential (mV, μV), pressure (mmHg), acoustic (dB), optical (transmittance), chemical concentration (mmol/L), or mechanical (force, displacement). In clinical and engineering practice, the most continuously recorded and analyzed are electrophysiological and cardiopulmonary signals.

For any biosignal, the BME engineer should always ask three questions: (1) Where does it come from? (cell / tissue / system-level generator); (2) How big and how fast? (typical amplitude, bandwidth, required sampling rate); (3) What contaminates it? (noise sources, common-mode, drift). This chapter builds that panoramic table.

💡
命名約定：名稱結尾的「-gram」代表記錄圖（記錄結果），如 ECG = Electrocardiogram；「-graph」則指儀器或記錄行為（如 electrocardiograph）。臨床日常用 ECG（美式）或 EKG（源自德文 Elektrokardiogramm）皆可互通。 Naming: The "-gram" suffix denotes the record (ECG = Electrocardiogram), while "-graph" denotes the instrument. In clinical use, ECG (American) and EKG (from German Elektrokardiogramm) are interchangeable.

電極技術

三、電極類型

電極是「離子電流（皮膚側）」與「電子電流（電路側）」的轉換器，本質上是電化學介面。不同電極選擇直接決定訊號品質、舒適度、長期可用性與成本。

An electrode is a transducer between "ionic current (skin side)" and "electronic current (circuit side)" — an electrochemical interface. The choice directly determines signal quality, comfort, long-term wearability, and cost.

🟢

① Ag/AgCl 凝膠電極

銀–氯化銀界面提供穩定的半電池電位，低極化、低漂移；搭配導電水膠（hydrogel）降低皮膚阻抗到 5–50 kΩ。臨床 ECG/EEG/EMG 通用，但拋棄式、有過敏風險、長期佩戴會乾化。

The silver/silver-chloride interface provides a stable half-cell potential, low polarization, low drift; combined with conductive hydrogel it drops skin impedance to 5–50 kΩ. Universal in clinical ECG/EEG/EMG, but disposable, allergen-risk, and dries out for long-term wear.

⚪

② Dry 電極

無凝膠，皮膚阻抗高（100 kΩ–1 MΩ），需配高輸入阻抗放大器（>10 GΩ）。優點：可重複使用、適合穿戴與長期使用；缺點：移動假影大、初接觸時 SNR 較差。

Gel-free; high skin impedance (100 kΩ–1 MΩ) demands very-high-input-impedance amps (>10 GΩ). Pros: reusable, ideal for wearables and long-term use. Cons: more motion artifact, worse SNR at initial contact.

🔵

③ 電容耦合電極

不直接接觸皮膚（隔衣服 / 介電材料），靠位移電流 i = C·dV/dt 耦合訊號。可整合進衣物、椅背、方向盤；對運動極敏感，需主動屏蔽（driven shield）。

No direct skin contact (through clothing / dielectric); couples via displacement current i = C·dV/dt. Integrable into clothing, car seats, steering wheels; highly motion-sensitive, requires active driven-shield.

🧵

④ 編織電極

把銀塗層、不鏽鋼絲或導電聚合物 PEDOT:PSS 編入紡織品。應用：智慧衣（Hexoskin、Astroskin）、嬰兒監測。可水洗 30–50 次後性能衰退。

Silver-coated fibers, stainless steel yarn, or PEDOT:PSS woven into fabric. Applications: smart shirts (Hexoskin, Astroskin), neonatal monitoring. Performance degrades after 30–50 washes.

🟣

⑤ 微電極陣列

10–100 μm 級陣列；應用：Utah array（神經元 single-unit）、HD-MEA（培養神經元）、Neuropixels（高密度 in vivo）。BCI 與神經科學主力工具。

10–100 μm scale arrays. Examples: Utah array (single-unit neurons in vivo), HD-MEA (cultured neurons), Neuropixels (high-density in vivo). The workhorse of BCI and neuroscience.

🟡

⑥ 表皮電極

厚度 μm、楊氏模量接近皮膚的金屬蛇形布線（serpentine）。能 7–14 天連續佩戴而不脫落；研究熱點：Rogers Lab、MC10 BioStamp。

μm-thin serpentine metal traces with skin-matched Young's modulus; wears 7–14 days continuously. Research focus: Rogers Lab, MC10 BioStamp.

標準導程

五、標準導程系統

🫀 Einthoven 三角 + 12 導程

1903 年 Einthoven 在右臂（RA）、左臂（LA）、左腿（LL）放電極，定義雙極導程：I = LA − RA、II = LL − RA、III = LL − LA，三者滿足 II = I + III。後續 Goldberger 加上 aVR/aVL/aVF（加壓單極），Wilson 加上 V1–V6（胸前單極），組成標準 12 導程。其中：

  • 下壁（II/III/aVF）：右冠狀動脈灌流區
  • 側壁（I/aVL/V5/V6）：左旋支
  • 前壁（V1–V4）：左前降支 LAD

In 1903 Einthoven placed electrodes on RA, LA, LL and defined bipolar leads I = LA−RA, II = LL−RA, III = LL−LA, satisfying II = I + III. Goldberger added aVR/aVL/aVF (augmented unipolar), Wilson added V1–V6 (precordial unipolar) → standard 12-lead ECG. Walls:

  • Inferior (II/III/aVF): RCA territory
  • Lateral (I/aVL/V5/V6): LCx
  • Anterior (V1–V4): LAD

🧠 10-20 EEG 系統

1958 年 Jasper 提出，把鼻根（nasion）到枕外隆突（inion）的距離分為 10% 與 20% 間距、共 21 個電極：Fp1/Fp2/F3/F4/F7/F8/Fz/C3/C4/Cz/T3/T4/T5/T6/P3/P4/Pz/O1/O2/A1/A2。奇數=左、偶數=右、z=中線。高密度版本（10-10、10-5）可達 64–256 通道。

Proposed by Jasper in 1958; 21 electrodes spaced at 10% and 20% of the nasion-to-inion distance: Fp1/Fp2/F3/F4/F7/F8/Fz/C3/C4/Cz/T3/T4/T5/T6/P3/P4/Pz/O1/O2/A1/A2. Odd = left, even = right, z = midline. Higher-density (10-10, 10-5) reach 64–256 channels.

🩺 BIPM 血壓協定

國際度量衡局（BIPM）與 ISO 81060-2:2019/AMD1:2024 規範非侵入式血壓計的準確度：與聽診標準同步量測，平均誤差 ±5 mmHg、標準差 ≤ 8 mmHg。AAMI/ESH/ISO 三方協定 2018 更新後為國際金標準。

BIPM and ISO 81060-2:2019/AMD1:2024 govern non-invasive BP device accuracy: simultaneous auscultatory reference, mean error ±5 mmHg, SD ≤ 8 mmHg. The 2018 AAMI/ESH/ISO joint protocol is the global gold standard.

💪 SENIAM 表面肌電指引

歐盟 SENIAM 計畫定義 30+ 肌肉的標準電極位置、電極間距（20 mm）、皮膚準備（去角質至 5 kΩ 以下）；是表面肌電（sEMG）的事實標準。

EU SENIAM project standardized electrode placement for 30+ muscles, inter-electrode distance (20 mm), and skin prep (exfoliate to < 5 kΩ); de-facto standard for sEMG.

決策樹

七、訊號選擇決策樹

🌳 臨床問題 → 生醫訊號

Q1.想偵測心律不整 / 心肌梗塞 / QT 延長？→ ECG（12 導程診斷級或單導 Holter / patch 連續監測）。Detecting arrhythmia / MI / QT prolongation? → ECG (12-lead diagnostic, or single-lead Holter / patch for ambulatory).
Q2.想偵測癲癇發作 / 睡眠分期 / 麻醉深度？→ EEG（10-20 系統，至少 8 通道；BIS™ 麻醉用 4 通道）。Detecting seizures / sleep stages / anesthesia depth? → EEG (10-20 system, ≥ 8 ch; BIS™ uses 4 ch for anesthesia).
Q3.想量肌肉疲勞 / 義肢控制意圖 / 神經肌肉病變？→ EMG（表面 sEMG 量肌群、針極 iEMG 量單一運動單元）。Measuring fatigue / prosthesis intent / neuromuscular disease? → EMG (sEMG for muscle groups; needle iEMG for single MUAPs).
Q4.想量血氧 / 低血氧？→ SpO₂（脈搏血氧計，兩波長比值法；新生兒用足部 transmissive）。Measuring oxygenation / hypoxemia? → SpO₂ (pulse oximeter, two-wavelength ratio; neonatal uses foot transmissive).
Q5.想監測連續血壓 + 心率變異但希望非侵入？→ PPG + 機器學習估算（waveform-based BP 估計）；金標準仍為動脈導管。Continuous BP + HRV non-invasively? → PPG + ML-based estimation; gold standard remains arterial line.
Q6.想做運動 / 居家 / 24×7 監測？→ 穿戴式 PPG（手錶/環）+ 1-lead ECG（patch）；避免單純依賴胸前帶。Sports / home / 24×7 monitoring? → Wearable PPG (watch/ring) + 1-lead ECG patch; avoid relying solely on chest straps.
Q7.想做胎兒心率監測？→ 母體腹部 fECG（需與母體 ECG 分離，常用 ICA）或都卜勒超音波（CTG）。Fetal heart-rate? → Maternal abdominal fECG (separate from maternal ECG via ICA) or Doppler ultrasound (CTG).
程式示例

八、ECG 合成與 QRS 偵測

1985 年 Jiapu Pan & Willis Tompkins 提出的 Pan-Tompkins 演算法，是工業界最常被引用的 QRS 偵測流程：(1) 5–15 Hz 帶通 → (2) 微分 → (3) 平方 → (4) 移動視窗積分（150 ms） → (5) 雙閾值 + 不應期。下面是 Python（scipy.signal）與 MATLAB（Signal Processing Toolbox）的最小可執行範例。

The 1985 Pan-Tompkins algorithm (Jiapu Pan & Willis Tompkins) is the industry's most-cited QRS detection pipeline: (1) 5–15 Hz band-pass → (2) differentiate → (3) square → (4) 150 ms moving-window integration → (5) dual thresholds + refractory. Minimal Python (scipy.signal) and MATLAB (Signal Processing Toolbox) examples below.

import numpy as np
from scipy.signal import butter, filtfilt, find_peaks

fs = 500            # sampling rate (Hz), IEC 60601-2-25 diagnostic
T  = 10; hr = 75
t  = np.arange(0, T, 1/fs)
rr = 60/hr

# --- Synthesize PQRST using Gaussian mixture per beat ---
def beat(tau):
    g = lambda a,c,w: a*np.exp(-((tau-c)/w)**2)
    return (g(0.15,0.15,0.025) - g(0.10,0.20,0.015)
          + g(1.20,0.22,0.012) - g(0.25,0.24,0.015)
          + g(0.30,0.40,0.045))
ecg = np.zeros_like(t)
for k in range(int(T/rr)+1):
    ecg += beat(t - k*rr)
ecg += 0.05*np.random.randn(len(t))                # white noise
ecg += 0.10*np.sin(2*np.pi*50*t)               # 50 Hz powerline

# --- Pan-Tompkins pre-processing ---
b,a = butter(2, [5/(fs/2), 15/(fs/2)], btype='band')
x   = filtfilt(b, a, ecg)
d   = np.diff(x, prepend=0)         # differentiate
s   = d**2                          # square
w   = np.ones(int(0.150*fs))/int(0.150*fs)
mwi = np.convolve(s, w, mode='same')
peaks,_ = find_peaks(mwi, distance=int(0.25*fs),
                     height=0.4*np.max(mwi))
print("Detected HR (bpm) =",
      round(60/np.mean(np.diff(peaks)/fs), 1))
fs = 500; T = 10; hr = 75;
t  = 0:1/fs:T-1/fs;  rr = 60/hr;
g  = @(a,c,w,tt) a*exp(-((tt-c)/w).^2);

ecg = zeros(1, length(t));
for k = 0:floor(T/rr)
    tau = t - k*rr;
    ecg = ecg + g(0.15,0.15,0.025,tau) - g(0.10,0.20,0.015,tau) ...
              + g(1.20,0.22,0.012,tau) - g(0.25,0.24,0.015,tau) ...
              + g(0.30,0.40,0.045,tau);
end
ecg = ecg + 0.05*randn(1,length(t)) + 0.10*sin(2*pi*50*t);

% Pan-Tompkins pre-processing
[b,a] = butter(2, [5 15]/(fs/2), 'bandpass');
x   = filtfilt(b,a,ecg);
d   = [0 diff(x)];
s   = d.^2;
mwi = movmean(s, round(0.150*fs));
[~,locs] = findpeaks(mwi, 'MinPeakDistance', round(0.25*fs), ...
                          'MinPeakHeight', 0.4*max(mwi));
fprintf('Detected HR = %.1f bpm\n', 60/mean(diff(locs)/fs));
關鍵術語

九、關鍵術語

CMRRCommon-Mode Rejection Ratio — 差動放大器抑制共模雜訊的能力，dB；生醫前端通常 ≥ 100 dB。   DRLDriven-Right-Leg — 把共模訊號反相注入右腿，主動降低工頻干擾。   SNRSignal-to-Noise Ratio — 訊噪比，常以 dB 表示。   Sampling rate (fs)每秒取樣次數，Hz；需 ≥ 2× 最高頻率 (Nyquist)。   Half-cell potential電極與電解液界面的平衡電位，材料相關；Ag/AgCl 約 220 mV。   Skin impedance皮膚對交流電的阻抗，常用 10 Hz 量；< 5 kΩ 為佳。   PQRSTECG 一個心動週期的五個波峰波谷：P（房去極）、QRS（室去極）、T（室復極）。   HRVHeart Rate Variability — 相鄰 R-R 間期變異；時域 SDNN/RMSSD、頻域 LF/HF。   10-20 systemEEG 頭皮電極標準座標系統；Jasper 1958。   Refractory period心臟細胞除極後 ~ 250 ms 內無法再除極；QRS 偵測同樣設這個守則避免偽峰。
