CHAPTER E1 · BLOCK E EMERGING & REGULATION

神經工程與腦機介面

從人工耳蝸到 Neuralink、從 DBS 到 Stentrode——神經工程把工程方法接到神經系統，用以「恢復、增強、治療」。本章帶你看遍記錄與刺激模態、BCI 解碼管線，以及 2024–2026 最新臨床進展。

From cochlear implants to Neuralink, from DBS to Stentrode — neural engineering interfaces with the nervous system to restore, augment, or treat. We tour recording and stimulation modalities, BCI decoding pipelines, and 2024–2026 clinical progress.

定義

一、什麼是神經工程？

神經工程（Neural Engineering）是把工程方法（電子、訊號、材料、機器學習）與神經科學結合，設計能與神經系統（中樞與周邊）建立資訊或能量交換的裝置、電極、演算法與療法。臨床目標可粗分三類：(1) 恢復 restoration——人工耳蝸恢復聽力、視網膜植入恢復視覺、BCI 恢復運動或語言；(2) 增強 augmentation——閉迴路認知/感覺輔助；(3) 治療 treatment——DBS 對帕金森、RNS 對癲癇、SCS 對慢性疼痛、VNS 對憂鬱。

神經工程涵蓋三大工程動作：記錄（recording）神經電/磁/光訊號、刺激（stimulation）注入電流或光以調控神經活動，以及解碼/編碼（decoding / encoding）把神經訊號翻譯成意圖（或反向）。每一個動作都對應特定電極、放大器、訊號處理、機器學習演算法、生物相容性與法規流程。

Neural engineering couples engineering disciplines (electronics, signals, materials, ML) with neuroscience to build devices, electrodes, algorithms and therapies that exchange information or energy with the nervous system (central and peripheral). Clinical goals fall into three buckets: (1) restoration — cochlear implants for hearing, retinal implants for vision, BCIs for motor/speech; (2) augmentation — closed-loop sensory/cognitive aids; (3) treatment — DBS for Parkinson's, RNS for epilepsy, SCS for chronic pain, VNS for depression.

Three engineering verbs dominate: recording neural electrical/magnetic/optical signals, stimulation via current or light to modulate activity, and decoding/encoding translating signals into intent (or the other way). Each requires its own electrodes, amplifiers, DSP, ML, biocompatibility, and regulatory path.

💡
動作電位回顧（Hodgkin-Huxley 1952）：神經元利用 Na⁺ / K⁺ 通道的電壓相依開合產生 ~1 ms、~100 mV 的動作電位，沿軸突以 1–120 m/s 傳遞。單神經元 spikes（>300 Hz 高頻成分）需穿刺電極或微電極陣列才能擷取；局部場電位 LFP（1–300 Hz）反映數千神經元突觸活動的總和，可由 ECoG/sEEG 擷取；頭皮 EEG（0.5–80 Hz）則是經頭骨衰減後的群體訊號。 Action-potential refresher (Hodgkin-Huxley, 1952): Neurons use voltage-gated Na⁺/K⁺ channels to fire ~1 ms, ~100 mV spikes along axons at 1–120 m/s. Single-unit spikes (>300 Hz components) need penetrating microelectrodes; local field potentials (LFP) (1–300 Hz) sum thousands of synapses and are captured by ECoG/sEEG; scalp EEG (0.5–80 Hz) is the population signal after skull attenuation.
記錄模態

二、神經訊號記錄模態比較

選擇模態的核心是「空間解析度 × 時間解析度 × 侵入性」的三角權衡。臨床上越深入越精細，但風險與成本越高。

The core trade-off is spatial resolution × temporal resolution × invasiveness. Going deeper buys precision but also risk and cost.

⚠️
慢性記錄穩定性是長年痛點：植入電極周邊 1–2 週開始形成 gliosis（神經膠細胞包覆）、6 個月後 spike yield 通常衰退 30–60%。Neuralink 等柔性電極（thread 直徑 ~4 μm，Young's modulus 接近腦組織）的設計動機，就是減少這個 foreign body response。 Chronic recording stability is a long-standing pain point. Implanted electrodes trigger gliosis within 1–2 weeks; spike yield typically falls 30–60% by 6 months. Flexible electrodes (Neuralink threads ~4 μm, Young's modulus close to brain tissue) are designed precisely to mitigate this foreign-body response.
刺激與神經調控

三、刺激與神經調控

刺激比記錄更接近「療法」。FDA 已核可的植入式神經調控覆蓋耳、腦、脊髓、迷走神經、視網膜等多個層次：

Stimulation is the "therapy" side. FDA-approved implants cover ear, brain, spinal cord, vagus nerve, and retina:

👂

人工耳蝸

把聲音先 FFT 分頻 → 對應到 22 條沿 cochlea 排列的電極（place coding）。三家主要廠商：Cochlear Ltd（Nucleus 8）、Advanced Bionics（Naída CI）、MED-EL（Sonnet 2）。編碼策略：CIS、ACE、MP3000；電流擴散 ~0.5 mm 為解析瓶頸。1980s 起，至今全球植入超過 100 萬例。

Sound is FFT-decomposed → 22 electrodes along the cochlea (place coding). Big three: Cochlear Ltd (Nucleus 8), Advanced Bionics (Naída CI), MED-EL (Sonnet 2). Strategies CIS, ACE, MP3000; current spread ~0.5 mm limits resolution. Since the 1980s, >1 million people have been implanted.

🧠

深部腦刺激（DBS）

STN/GPi/VIM/ANT 等靶點高頻刺激（~130 Hz）。Medtronic Activa / Percept PC（內建 BrainSense 偵測 β 振盪）、Boston Scientific Vercise Genus、Abbott Infinity（無方向性 / 方向性電極）。適應症：帕金森、原發性顫抖、肌張力不全、強迫症（OCD HDE）、難治性癲癇。

High-frequency (~130 Hz) stim of STN/GPi/VIM/ANT. Medtronic Activa / Percept PC (BrainSense β-band sensing), Boston Scientific Vercise Genus, Abbott Infinity (directional leads). Indications: Parkinson's, essential tremor, dystonia, OCD (HDE), refractory epilepsy.

👁️

視網膜植入

Argus II（Second Sight）2013 FDA 核准，2019 公司倒閉、2022 全面停止支援，使用者面對「植入物孤兒」議題。當代研究：Pixium Vision PRIMA 為 subretinal photovoltaic 陣列（無線、由近紅外驅動）、Science Bionic Eye 計畫、日本人工視網膜。挑戰：解析度仍遠低於正常視覺。

Argus II (Second Sight) approved 2013; company bankrupt 2019, support discontinued 2022, leaving "orphan implant" patients. Current efforts: Pixium Vision PRIMA (sub-retinal photovoltaic, NIR-driven), Science Bionic Eye, Japan's artificial retina. Resolution remains far below normal vision.

🦴

脊髓電刺激

Nevro HFX 10 kHz paresthesia-free、Boston Scientific WaveWriter Alpha、Abbott Proclaim、Medtronic Inceptiv（首支閉迴路 ECAP-sensing）。用於慢性背痛、糖尿病周邊神經痛、複雜性區域疼痛症候群（CRPS）。

Nevro HFX 10 kHz (paresthesia-free), Boston Scientific WaveWriter Alpha, Abbott Proclaim, Medtronic Inceptiv (first closed-loop ECAP sensing). For chronic back pain, painful diabetic neuropathy, CRPS.

🩺

迷走神經刺激

LivaNova（前 Cyberonics）VNS Therapy 用於難治性癲癇（1997）與憂鬱症（2005）。透過環狀電極包覆左頸迷走神經，傳入 NTS → locus coeruleus → 皮質擴散影響。2024 FDA 開放阻塞性睡眠呼吸中止症 obstructive sleep apnea（Inspire Medical 是不同產品，靶舌下神經）。

LivaNova (ex-Cyberonics) VNS Therapy for refractory epilepsy (1997) and depression (2005). Cuff electrode on the left cervical vagus → NTS → locus coeruleus → cortical modulation. Inspire Medical for OSA is a different device (hypoglossal nerve).

⚡

反應性神經刺激（RNS）

NeuroPace RNS System（2013 PMA）：閉迴路癲癇治療，植入顱骨內，偵測到 ictal pattern 即發送刺激中斷發作。是首支「sense-and-respond」腦植入；2023 NAUTILUS 試驗擴展全身性癲癇適應症。

NeuroPace RNS System (PMA 2013): closed-loop for epilepsy, embedded in the skull; detects ictal patterns and delivers counter-stimulation. The first "sense-and-respond" brain implant; 2023 NAUTILUS trial expanded to generalized epilepsy.

BCI 解碼

四、腦機介面解碼管線

BCI = 量測神經訊號 → 提取特徵 → 解碼成控制命令。經典「典範（paradigm）」分三大類：

  1. 運動想像（Motor Imagery）——使用者想像左手 / 右手 / 腳動作，激發 μ (8–12 Hz) 與 β (13–30 Hz) 的 event-related desynchronization (ERD)。對應到 Common Spatial Pattern (CSP) + LDA 是經典 EEG 解碼配方。
  2. P300——對「期待的、稀有的」刺激會在 ~300 ms 出現大正波，常見「字母矩陣 speller」。
  3. SSVEP（穩態視覺誘發電位）——對特定頻率閃爍刺激（6–30 Hz）的同頻反應，特點是訊號雜訊比高、訓練少。

植入式 BCI 用 Utah array 或 Neuralink threads 量單神經元 spikes 與 LFP，解碼器常見三代：(a) Kalman / Wiener filter（線性、可解釋），(b) RNN/LSTM，(c) Transformer（近年新主流；2023–2025 Stanford 與 UCSF 在語音 BCI 解碼上以 Transformer + CTC 大幅提升字錯誤率）。

BCI = measure neural signal → extract features → decode to control commands. Three classic paradigms:

  1. Motor Imagery — user imagines left/right hand or foot movement, eliciting μ (8–12 Hz) and β (13–30 Hz) ERD. Classic recipe: Common Spatial Pattern (CSP) + LDA on EEG.
  2. P300 — a ~300 ms positive deflection after a rare, expected stimulus; basis of the letter-matrix speller.
  3. SSVEP — synchronous response to a flickering stimulus (6–30 Hz); high SNR, minimal training.

Implanted BCIs use Utah arrays or Neuralink threads to capture single-unit spikes and LFP. Three generations of decoders: (a) Kalman/Wiener filter (linear, interpretable); (b) RNN/LSTM; (c) Transformer (current SOTA — Stanford and UCSF 2023–2025 showed Transformer + CTC slashed WER in speech BCIs).

決策樹

六、神經介面選擇決策樹

🌳 臨床問題 → 神經介面決策樹

Q1.病人有四肢麻痺、想恢復電腦/機器手臂控制？→ 高密度皮質內電極 BCI（Neuralink N1、Blackrock Utah、Paradromics）或經血管 BCI（Synchron Stentrode）。Patient has tetraplegia and wants to control computer / robotic arm? → Intracortical BCI (Neuralink N1, Blackrock Utah, Paradromics) or transvascular (Synchron Stentrode).
Q2.病人為重度感音性聾？→ 人工耳蝸（Cochlear / AB / MED-EL），術前需殘餘聽力與蝸管解剖評估。Patient has severe-profound sensorineural deafness? → Cochlear implant (Cochlear / AB / MED-EL); pre-op residual hearing + cochlear anatomy review.
Q3.病人為藥物難治帕金森、原發性顫抖？→ DBS（Medtronic Percept PC、Boston Sci Vercise、Abbott Infinity），靶點 STN/GPi/VIM 依症狀選擇。Patient has medication-refractory Parkinson's or essential tremor? → DBS (Percept PC / Vercise / Infinity); target STN/GPi/VIM by symptom.
Q4.病人為視網膜色素變性致盲？→ 仍在實驗階段（Pixium PRIMA、Science Bionic Eye）；尚無 FDA 批准商用產品（Argus II 已停產）。Patient has retinitis-pigmentosa blindness? → Still experimental (Pixium PRIMA, Science Bionic Eye); no FDA-approved commercial device (Argus II discontinued).
Q5.病人為難治性癲癇？→ 先 sEEG mapping → 切除或 RNS（NeuroPace）或 VNS（LivaNova）。Patient has refractory epilepsy? → sEEG mapping → resection or RNS (NeuroPace) or VNS (LivaNova).
Q6.病人為慢性背痛、複雜疼痛？→ SCS（Nevro HFX、Medtronic Inceptiv 閉迴路、Abbott Proclaim）。Patient has chronic back / complex pain? → SCS (Nevro HFX, Medtronic Inceptiv closed-loop, Abbott Proclaim).

程式示例

八、簡易 Spike 偵測

以下範例在模擬的神經訊號上做最簡單的 threshold-crossing 偵測，並回傳每個 spike 的時間索引。實務上會加入 refractory period（避免一個動作電位被算成多個）與 PCA + 群集進行 spike sorting。

The following example runs the simplest threshold-crossing spike detector on a simulated neural trace and returns each spike's sample index. Production pipelines add a refractory period (so a single AP isn't double-counted) plus PCA + clustering for full spike sorting.

import numpy as np
from scipy.signal import butter, filtfilt, find_peaks

fs  = 30_000                                # 30 kHz acquisition
T   = 2.0                                   # 2 s of data
t   = np.arange(0, T, 1/fs)
rng = np.random.default_rng(42)
sig = rng.normal(0, 15, t.size)         # 15 μV noise
for ts in rng.uniform(0.05, T-0.05, 40):    # inject spikes
    i = int(ts*fs)
    sig[i:i+30] += -80*np.exp(-np.arange(30)/8)

# Band-pass 0.3–7 kHz to isolate spike band
b, a   = butter(4, [300, 7000], "bandpass", fs=fs)
hp     = filtfilt(b, a, sig)

# Threshold = 5× MAD (Quiroga 2004 robust estimator)
thr    = 5*np.median(np.abs(hp))/0.6745
spikes, _ = find_peaks(-hp, height=thr, distance=int(0.001*fs))   # 1 ms refractory
print(f"Detected {len(spikes)} spikes; threshold = {thr:.1f} μV")
% Minimal spike detection in MATLAB
fs = 30e3; T = 2; t = 0:1/fs:T-1/fs;
rng(42);
sig = 15*randn(1, length(t));
ts  = 0.05 + (T-0.1)*rand(1,40);
for k = 1:length(ts)
    i = round(ts(k)*fs);
    sig(i:i+29) = sig(i:i+29) - 80*exp(-(0:29)/8);
end

% Band-pass 0.3–7 kHz
[b,a] = butter(4, [300 7000]/(fs/2), 'bandpass');
hp    = filtfilt(b, a, sig);

thr   = 5*median(abs(hp))/0.6745;
[~, locs] = findpeaks(-hp, 'MinPeakHeight', thr, ...
                       'MinPeakDistance', round(0.001*fs));
fprintf('Detected %d spikes; thr = %.1f uV\n', length(locs), thr);
工程挑戰

九、神經介面工程挑戰

  1. 慢性記錄穩定性 — FBR、gliosis、encapsulation；柔性電極、塗層（PEDOT:PSS、parylene-C）、機器人植入精準度都是研發方向。Foreign-body response, gliosis, encapsulation; mitigation via flexible electrodes, coatings (PEDOT:PSS, parylene-C), precise robotic placement.
  2. 無線供電與資料 — 經顱感應耦合、超音波 power、UWB 高頻寬資料；功率密度受 IEEE C95 / IEC 60601-2-33 限制（避免熱傷害）。Transcutaneous inductive coupling, ultrasound power, UWB high-bandwidth data; power density limited by IEEE C95 / IEC 60601-2-33 to prevent thermal injury.
  3. 生物相容性 — ISO 10993 系列；植入超過 30 天屬 long-term，cytotoxicity、sensitization、implantation、genotoxicity、chronic toxicity 皆需做。ISO 10993 series; implant duration >30 days = long-term, requiring cytotoxicity, sensitization, implantation, genotoxicity, chronic toxicity testing.
  4. 法規路徑 — 植入式神經調控絕大多數為 Class III + PMA；輔以 IDE 臨床研究、Breakthrough Device 路徑。EU MDR 下為 Class III + Annex IX。Implantable neuromodulators are almost all Class III + PMA; supported by IDE clinical study, Breakthrough Device pathway. EU MDR: Class III + Annex IX.
  5. 網路安全 — 2025/9 FDA cybersecurity 指南終版生效；BCI 與 DBS 因含無線通訊與韌體更新，須符合 SPDF (Secure Product Development Framework)。FDA cybersecurity final guidance effective Sep 2025; BCIs and DBSes (with wireless + firmware update) must follow SPDF (Secure Product Development Framework).
  6. 倫理與資料主權 — 「神經權利 neurorights」概念興起；智利 2021 修憲為全球首例；Chile, Brazil, Colorado 2024 立法限制神經資料商業使用。"Neurorights" gaining traction; Chile amended its constitution in 2021 (world first); Brazil, Colorado 2024 laws restrict commercial use of neural data.
關鍵術語

十、本章關鍵術語

BCIBrain-Computer Interface — 直接以神經訊號控制外部裝置或溝通的系統。   ECoGElectrocorticography — 放在腦皮質表面的陣列電極，空間 2–10 mm。   Utah array10×10 矽針陣列、400 μm 間距、100 通道；BrainGate 計畫核心硬體。   DBSDeep Brain Stimulation — 植入電極對 STN/GPi/VIM 等核團高頻刺激。   RNSResponsive Neurostimulation — 閉迴路偵測 + 即時刺激；NeuroPace 為代表。   SCSSpinal Cord Stimulation — 脊髓硬膜外電極刺激緩解慢性疼痛。   VNSVagus Nerve Stimulation — 環狀電極包覆迷走神經；LivaNova 用於癲癇與憂鬱。   CISContinuous Interleaved Sampling — 人工耳蝸的經典編碼策略，避免電極間電流交互。   CSPCommon Spatial Pattern — EEG 運動想像 BCI 的經典空間濾波器。   P300面對稀有、預期刺激約 300 ms 出現的正向 ERP，常用於 BCI speller。   SSVEPSteady-State Visual Evoked Potential — 對閃爍刺激的同頻 EEG 反應。   Gliosis電極周圍神經膠細胞增生形成的包覆層，是慢性記錄退化的主因。   PEDOT:PSS導電聚合物，常作神經電極塗層降低阻抗、提升生物相容性。
