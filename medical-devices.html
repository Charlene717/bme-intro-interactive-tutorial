CHAPTER D1 · BLOCK D DEVICES & INTERFACES

醫療器材設計與分類

從 Class I 牙線到 Class III 心律調節器、從 510(k) 到 PMA、從 QMSR 到 EU MDR、從 IEC 60601 到 UDI——本章帶你建立全球醫療器材法規地圖與設計流程。

From Class I dental floss to Class III pacemakers, 510(k) to PMA, QMSR to EU MDR, IEC 60601 to UDI — build a global map of medical device regulation and the design lifecycle.

定義

一、什麼是醫療器材？

依美國 FDA《食品藥物化妝品法》(FD&C Act) Section 201(h) 的定義，醫療器材是「用於診斷、治療、緩解、預防疾病，或影響身體結構/功能的儀器、機械、植入物、體外試劑、軟體」——且其主要作用不是經由化學/代謝路徑達成（否則屬於藥品 drug）。

關鍵分界：藥品（drug）以化學作用治病、生物製劑（biologic）以細胞/蛋白質作用、醫療器材（device）以物理/機械/電子/光學作用達成診療目的。實務上有「組合產品 combination product」例外：藥物洗脫支架（drug-eluting stent）、自動注射筆、預充式注射器、CAR-T 製造設備皆屬之。

Per US FDA's FD&C Act Section 201(h), a medical device is "an instrument, apparatus, implant, in-vitro reagent, or software intended for diagnosis, cure, mitigation, treatment, or prevention of disease, or for altering body structure/function" — and whose primary mode of action is not chemical or metabolic (otherwise it's a drug).

Key distinction: drugs act chemically, biologics act via cells/proteins, devices act physically, mechanically, electrically, or optically. "Combination products" sit in the middle: drug-eluting stents, auto-injectors, prefilled syringes, CAR-T manufacturing equipment.

💡
SaMD（Software as a Medical Device）：2017 年起 IMDRF 正式承認軟體本身可作為醫療器材，不需綁定硬體；典型例子如 Apple Watch AFib 偵測 App、Caption Health AI 超音波導引、IDx-DR 糖尿病視網膜病變診斷。FDA 將其與一般醫療軟體分開監管。 SaMD (Software as a Medical Device): Since 2017 IMDRF formally recognizes software itself as a medical device — no hardware tether required. Examples: Apple Watch AFib detection app, Caption Health AI ultrasound guidance, IDx-DR diabetic retinopathy diagnosis. FDA regulates SaMD separately from in-device software.
風險分類

二、FDA 三級風險分類

FDA 依「對使用者/病人造成傷害的風險」把醫療器材分為三級。風險越高，上市要求越嚴格。

FDA classifies devices into three tiers by the risk of harm to the user/patient. Higher risk → stricter market-entry requirements.

級別風險管制代表器材上市路徑
Class I低一般管制牙線、繃帶、聽診器多數 510(k) 豁免
Class II中一般 + 特殊管制輸液泵、X 光、CT、超音波510(k) 為主
Class III高一般 + 特殊 + PMA心律調節器、ICD、DES、人工瓣膜、DBSPMA
⚠️
510(k) 豁免 ≠ 不需註冊。Class I 多數豁免 510(k) 上市前審查，但仍須完成 FDA establishment registration、device listing、遵守 QMSR/GMP、不良事件 MDR 通報、標籤要求。 510(k)-exempt ≠ unregulated. Most Class I devices are exempt from premarket 510(k) review, but must still complete FDA establishment registration, device listing, comply with QMSR/GMP, report adverse events (MDR), and meet labeling.
上市路徑

三、FDA 五大上市路徑

📋

510(k) — 實質等同

適用 Class II 與少數 Class III。需證明與已上市的「predicate device」實質等同（substantial equivalence）——相同用途 + 相同/相似技術特徵。FDA 每年核可 10,000+ 件，平均審查 90–180 天，使用者規費 2025 約 USD 24,335（標準）/ 6,083（小公司）。

For Class II and a few Class III. Must demonstrate substantial equivalence to a marketed "predicate device" — same intended use + same/similar technological characteristics. FDA clears 10,000+ per year, average review 90–180 days; FY2025 user fee ~USD 24,335 (standard) / 6,083 (small business).

🆕

De Novo — 新型低/中風險

2018 年 FDARA 制度化的路徑：新穎的低/中風險裝置但無 predicate 可比，避免被迫走 PMA。經 De Novo 獲准後即建立新的 generic category，後續同類產品可走 510(k)。例：IDx-DR（首支 AI 診斷）、Apple Watch ECG（2018）。

Codified by 2018 FDARA: novel low/moderate-risk devices with no predicate, avoiding forced PMA. Once granted, De Novo establishes a new generic category — subsequent similar products can use 510(k). Examples: IDx-DR (first AI diagnostic), Apple Watch ECG (2018).

🏥

PMA — 上市前核准

Class III 的「黃金標準」路徑：需獨立臨床試驗證明安全有效；2025 標準規費 USD ~520,000（小公司 ~130,000）。審查時間 1–3 年；通過後產品稱為 PMA-approved（非 cleared）。例：HeartMate III LVAD、Tandem t:slim X2 with Control-IQ。

Gold-standard pathway for Class III: requires independent clinical trial proving safety + effectiveness; FY2025 standard fee ~USD 520k (small business ~130k). Review 1–3 yr; products are PMA-approved (not cleared). Examples: HeartMate III LVAD, Tandem t:slim X2 with Control-IQ.

💎

HDE — 人道醫療豁免

Humanitarian Device Exemption：罕見疾病（年盛行率 ≤ 8,000 美國人口）裝置的縮減版 PMA，只需證明「合理安全性 + 可能受益」。例：兒童 LVAD Berlin Heart EXCOR、視網膜植入 Argus II（已退市）。

For rare-disease devices (US prevalence ≤8,000/yr): a reduced PMA requiring "reasonable safety + probable benefit." Examples: Berlin Heart EXCOR pediatric VAD, Argus II retinal implant (discontinued).

🧬

HCT/Ps — 人體細胞/組織

21 CFR 1271 規範：「最低操作 minimal manipulation + 同源使用 homologous use」者走 361 路徑（無需上市前審查）；否則須走 BLA。例：心臟同種異體瓣膜、骨同種異體移植；不適用 CAR-T（屬 BLA）。

21 CFR 1271 framework: "minimally manipulated + homologously used" go through 361 pathway (no premarket review); otherwise need BLA. Examples: allograft heart valves, allograft bone; CAR-T does NOT qualify (it's a BLA).

⚡

EUA — 緊急使用授權

公共衛生緊急狀態下啟用（COVID-19、Mpox）。FDA Commissioner 可在沒有完整 510(k)/PMA 下授權；COVID 期間累計核可 2,400+ 件診斷與裝置。緊急狀態結束後須轉正規路徑或下市。

Activated during public-health emergency (COVID-19, Mpox). FDA Commissioner can authorize without full 510(k)/PMA; 2,400+ COVID diagnostics/devices were issued EUAs. Must transition to regular pathway or be withdrawn after emergency ends.

QMSR

四、QSR → QMSR：FDA 與 ISO 13485 整合

21 CFR Part 820（Quality System Regulation, QSR）自 1996 年起為美國醫療器材品質管理的法源。但與全球業界遵循的 ISO 13485:2016 重複度高、術語不一致，造成出口廠商雙重負擔。

2024 年 2 月 2 日，FDA 公告 final rule，將 21 CFR Part 820 改名為 Quality Management System Regulation (QMSR)，明文「by reference」incorporates ISO 13485:2016，加上少量 FDA 特殊要求（如 MDR 不良事件通報、UDI、records 部分）。生效日為 2026 年 2 月 2 日，給業界 2 年轉換期。

  • 術語對齊：Design History File (DHF) ≈ Medical Device File (MDF)；Device Master Record (DMR) ≈ MDF；management responsibility 用 ISO 章節編號。
  • 原 21 CFR 820 大幅刪減（820.30 Design Controls、820.50 Purchasing 等併入 ISO 7.3/7.4）。
  • 影響：跨國公司可使用單一 QMS 系統；ISO 13485 認證稽核員需熟悉 FDA 特殊條款；新 inspection 模式 ISO 13485-aligned。

21 CFR Part 820 (Quality System Regulation, QSR) has governed US medical device quality since 1996. But it overlapped heavily with industry's global standard ISO 13485:2016, causing duplication for exporters.

On 2024-02-02 FDA published a final rule renaming 21 CFR Part 820 to Quality Management System Regulation (QMSR), formally "by reference" incorporating ISO 13485:2016, plus a few FDA-specific extras (MDR reporting, UDI, records). Effective 2026-02-02, giving industry a 2-year transition.

  • Terminology aligned: Design History File (DHF) ≈ Medical Device File (MDF); Device Master Record (DMR) ≈ MDF; management responsibility uses ISO chapter numbering.
  • Much of old 21 CFR 820 deleted (820.30 Design Controls, 820.50 Purchasing → ISO 7.3/7.4).
  • Impact: multinationals can run a single QMS; ISO 13485 auditors must learn FDA add-ons; new inspection model is ISO 13485-aligned.
全球法規

五、其他主要法規體系

🇪🇺 EU MDR 2017/745

2017 年通過、2021/5/26 生效，取代舊版 MDD 93/42/EEC。核心改變：(1) 重新分類，許多 MDD 的 Class IIa/IIb 升級為 III；(2) UDI 強制；(3) PMS / PMCF 上市後監測與臨床追蹤強制；(4) Eudamed 資料庫；(5) Notified Body 稽核更嚴；(6) CE marking。MDCG 指引文件持續發布。

Adopted 2017, effective 2021-05-26; replaced MDD 93/42/EEC. Key changes: (1) reclassification (many MDD IIa/IIb → III); (2) mandatory UDI; (3) mandatory PMS / PMCF; (4) Eudamed database; (5) stricter Notified Body audits; (6) CE marking. MDCG guidance documents updated continuously.

🇯🇵 PMDA（日本）

Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Agency，依《藥機法》分四級（I/II/III/IV）。Class I 屆出（self-declaration）、Class II 多數由 PMDA 認證機構處理、Class III/IV 由 PMDA 本部審查。再生醫療有獨立「條件期限承認」快速通道（2014 起）。

Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Agency, four classes (I–IV) under PMD Act. Class I: self-declaration; Class II: mostly Registered Certification Bodies; Class III/IV: PMDA HQ. Regenerative medicine has its own conditional/term-limited approval fast track (since 2014).

🇨🇳 NMPA（中國）

National Medical Products Administration（原 CFDA），三級分類。Class I 屆出、II 省級審查、III 中央審查。2017 加入 ICH；2020/2021 醫療器材監督管理條例改革；2024 起接受 RWE 真實世界資料；本土創新通道加速。

National Medical Products Administration (formerly CFDA), three-class system. Class I: filing; II: provincial; III: central. Joined ICH in 2017; 2020/2021 device regulation reform; from 2024 RWE accepted; domestic innovation pathway accelerated.

🇹🇼 TFDA（台灣）

衛福部食品藥物管理署，依《醫療器材管理法》（2021/5/1 施行）分三級。多採 reliance / harmonization 路徑（參考 FDA / EU / Japan 核可可加速）；GMP 認可 ISO 13485。SaMD、AI 醫療裝置近年新增專案審查。

MOHW TFDA, three-class system under Medical Device Act (effective 2021-05-01). Often uses reliance / harmonization (FDA/EU/Japan approvals accelerate review); ISO 13485 accepted as GMP. Recent dedicated review tracks for SaMD and AI devices.

🇨🇦 Health Canada

Medical Devices Regulations (MDR, SOR/98-282)，四級分類（I/II/III/IV）。Class II–IV 需 Medical Device Licence (MDL)；要求 MDSAP 認證（單一稽核覆蓋加/美/巴/澳/日）。Class I 只需 Establishment Licence (MDEL)。

Medical Devices Regulations (MDR, SOR/98-282), four classes (I–IV). Class II–IV need a Medical Device Licence (MDL); MDSAP certification required (single audit covers CA/US/BR/AU/JP). Class I needs only an Establishment Licence (MDEL).

🇦🇺 TGA（澳洲）

Therapeutic Goods Administration。器材依風險分 Class I → IV + Active Implantable + IVD。多用 EU CE 或 MDSAP 報告加速。SaMD 自 2021/2 起特別規範；2024 起 AI/ML SaMD 上市後變更新指引。

Therapeutic Goods Administration. Devices classified I → IV + Active Implantable + IVD. Often accepts EU CE or MDSAP reports for acceleration. SaMD specifically regulated since 2021-02; 2024 new guidance on AI/ML SaMD post-market changes.

IEC 60601

六、IEC 60601 標準家族

所有「插電 / 電池供電」的醫療器材都必須通過 IEC 60601 系列認證。FDA 把 60601-1 列為 recognized consensus standard；EU MDR 透過 harmonized standard 機制承認。

Every "powered" medical device (mains or battery) must comply with the IEC 60601 family. FDA recognizes 60601-1 as a consensus standard; EU MDR recognizes it via harmonized-standard mechanism.

標準範疇重點
IEC 60601-1一般要求（Ed. 3.2 / Ed. 4 在制定中）漏電流、絕緣、耐壓、應用部分 B/BF/CF
IEC 60601-1-2電磁相容Ed. 4.1 (2020)；wireless 共存
IEC 60601-1-6可用性對齊 IEC 62366
IEC 60601-1-8警報系統優先順序、視聽特徵
IEC 60601-1-9環境設計生命週期環境
IEC 60601-1-11家用環境家用設備
IEC 60601-2-25心電圖（collateral）ECG 效能
IEC 60601-2-24輸液泵流速、occlusion
IEC 60601-2-4去顫器能量、波形
💡
應用部分（Applied Part）三分類：
• Type B：非直接電氣連接病人（檢查燈、推床）；
• Type BF：直接接觸但不接心臟（ECG 電極、血壓袖帶）；漏電流 ≤ 100 μA；
• Type CF：可接觸心臟或心血管系統（侵入式血壓 IBP、心導管 catheter）；漏電流 ≤ 10 μA，最嚴格。
判別圖示分別為人形 (B)、人形帶框 (BF)、心臟 (CF)。 Applied Part 3 types:
• Type B: no direct electrical contact (exam lamp, bed);
• Type BF: direct skin contact, no heart (ECG, BP cuff); leakage ≤ 100 μA;
• Type CF: cardiac/cardiovascular contact (invasive BP, catheter); leakage ≤ 10 μA, strictest.

決策樹

七、上市路徑決策樹

🌳 FDA 上市路徑決策樹

Q1.是「不維持生命、不造成不合理風險」的低風險器材（如棉花棒、繃帶）？→ Class I，多數 510(k) 豁免，但仍需 establishment registration + listing。Low-risk, non-life-sustaining (cotton swabs, bandages)? → Class I, mostly 510(k)-exempt; still need establishment registration + listing.
Q2.有 predicate device（同用途、同技術）？→ 510(k)，平均 90–180 天 cleared。Has a predicate device (same use + same tech)? → 510(k), average 90–180 days clearance.
Q3.沒有 predicate 但風險中/低、可由 special controls 管理？→ De Novo，建立新 generic category。No predicate but moderate/low risk, manageable by special controls? → De Novo, creates new generic category.
Q4.維持生命、預防重大傷害（心律調節器、人工瓣膜）？→ Class III / PMA，需 pivotal clinical trial。Life-sustaining / supports critical function (pacemaker, valve)? → Class III / PMA, needs pivotal clinical trial.
Q5.針對罕病（年盛行率 ≤ 8,000）？→ HDE，可享規費減免與較寬鬆證據門檻。For rare disease (US prevalence ≤ 8,000/yr)? → HDE, fee waivers + lower evidence bar.
Q6.純軟體不含硬體？→ SaMD，分類依 IMDRF 框架（State of healthcare situation × Significance of information）。Software only, no hardware? → SaMD, classify by IMDRF framework (situation × significance).

生命週期

十、生命週期與召回

醫療器材生命週期 = 「設計 → 製造 → 上市 → 監控 → 召回/退市」全程受 FDA / 競爭機關監管。

  1. 設計管制 21 CFR 820.30 — 需建立 DHF（Design History File）紀錄所有 design input/output/V&V/risk。
  2. 製造管制（DMR, Device Master Record） — 批次紀錄、QC 規格、IFU、標籤、UDI。
  3. MDR（Medical Device Reporting, 21 CFR 803） — 製造商須在 30 天內回報嚴重不良事件（死亡、重傷、可能再發）；5 日內回報需立即矯正的事件。
  4. 上市後監測 PMS — 收集真實世界使用資料；FDA Sentinel、MAUDE 公開資料庫。EU MDR 強制 PMCF（上市後臨床追蹤）。
  5. 召回分級：
    • Class I 召回：可能致死或重大傷害（最嚴重）
    • Class II：可能造成暫時性或可逆性健康問題
    • Class III：違規但不致造成健康問題

Device lifecycle = "design → manufacture → market → monitor → recall/withdrawal" — fully regulated by FDA / competent authorities.

  1. Design Controls 21 CFR 820.30 — DHF (Design History File) documents all design input/output/V&V/risk.
  2. Manufacturing (DMR, Device Master Record) — batch records, QC specs, IFU, labeling, UDI.
  3. MDR (Medical Device Reporting, 21 CFR 803) — manufacturer must report serious adverse events (death, serious injury, recurrence-possible) within 30 days; events needing immediate correction within 5 days.
  4. Post-market surveillance (PMS) — real-world use data; FDA Sentinel + public MAUDE database. EU MDR mandates PMCF.
  5. Recall classes:
    • Class I recall: may cause death/serious injury (most severe)
    • Class II: may cause temporary or reversible health problems
    • Class III: violation unlikely to cause health problems
🚨
個案：Philips Respironics CPAP / BiPAP 召回（2021）
2021 年 6 月 Philips 公告召回 1,500+ 萬台 CPAP/BiPAP/ventilator 含 polyester-based polyurethane (PE-PUR) 消音泡棉，可能降解釋出毒性微粒/化學物。FDA 列為 Class I 召回。Philips 後與美國司法部簽署 consent decree，賠償 11 億美元；Respironics 業務 2024 暫停美國銷售。教訓：材料生物相容性測試（ISO 10993）+ 加速老化測試的重要性。 Case: Philips Respironics CPAP/BiPAP recall (2021)
June 2021 Philips recalled 15M+ CPAP/BiPAP/ventilators containing PE-PUR sound-abatement foam that could degrade into toxic particles/chemicals. FDA classified as Class I recall. Philips later signed a consent decree with DOJ, paid $1.1B settlement; Respironics paused US sales 2024. Lesson: biocompatibility testing (ISO 10993) + accelerated aging are critical.
程式示例

十一、查詢 openFDA 510(k) 資料

openFDA 提供免費 REST API 查詢 510(k)、PMA、recall、device events 等。下例查詢某產品代碼最新 10 件 510(k) clearance。

openFDA provides a free REST API for 510(k), PMA, recalls, device events, and more. The example below queries the 10 most recent 510(k) clearances for a product code.

import requests, pandas as pd

# Product code DQA = Continuous Glucose Monitor
url = "https://api.fda.gov/device/510k.json"
params = {
    "search": "product_code:DQA",
    "sort":   "decision_date:desc",
    "limit":  10,
}
r = requests.get(url, params=params, timeout=30)
r.raise_for_status()
records = r.json()["results"]

df = pd.DataFrame([{
    "k_number":     x["k_number"],
    "applicant":    x["applicant"],
    "device_name":  x["device_name"],
    "decision":     x["decision_description"],
    "date":         x["decision_date"],
} for x in records])
print(df.head(10))
% openFDA 510(k) query in MATLAB
url = 'https://api.fda.gov/device/510k.json';
opts = weboptions('Timeout',30);
params = {'search','product_code:DQA', ...
          'sort',  'decision_date:desc', ...
          'limit', '10'};
data = webread(url, params{:}, opts);

T = struct2table(data.results);
disp(T(:, {'k_number','applicant','device_name','decision_date'}));
關鍵術語

十二、關鍵術語

510(k)FDA 上市前許可，需證明與已上市 predicate device 實質等同。   PMAPremarket Approval — Class III 高風險裝置嚴格上市路徑，需獨立臨床試驗。   De Novo創新低/中風險、無 predicate 的裝置可走的路徑，建立新 generic category。   HDEHumanitarian Device Exemption — 罕病裝置（年盛行率 ≤ 8,000）的縮減版 PMA。   QMSRQuality Management System Regulation — 取代 21 CFR 820 QSR，與 ISO 13485:2016 對齊；2026/2/2 生效。   UDIUnique Device Identification — 醫療器材全球唯一識別碼。   PMSPost-Market Surveillance — 上市後監測，蒐集真實世界資料、不良事件。   PMCFPost-Market Clinical Follow-up — EU MDR 強制的臨床追蹤。   EudamedEuropean Database on Medical Devices — EU MDR 中央資料庫。   MDR兩種意義：(1) EU Medical Device Regulation 2017/745；(2) US Medical Device Reporting (21 CFR 803)。   DHFDesign History File — 完整記錄設計歷程的文件包。   DMRDevice Master Record — 量產所需完整製造紀錄。   IEC 60601醫用電氣設備安全國際標準家族，含 EMC、可用性等 collateral 與 200+ 特定設備子標準。   Applied Part直接或間接接觸病人的器材部位，分 B / BF / CF 三類。
