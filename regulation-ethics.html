法規、倫理與品質系統

從 FDA QMSR、EU MDR、ISO 13485、ISO 14971，到 IEC 62304 Ed.2、GMLP、PCCP、EU AI Act、HIPAA、GDPR——本章把 BME 工程師必須一輩子記得的法規地圖一次串起。

From FDA QMSR, EU MDR, ISO 13485, and ISO 14971 to IEC 62304 Ed.2, GMLP, PCCP, the EU AI Act, HIPAA, and GDPR — this chapter links the regulatory map a BME engineer carries for life.

ISO & IEC

二、ISO / IEC 必懂標準

ISO 13485:2016

全球醫療器材品質管理系統標準。2025/3 確認、無修改；下次預計 2026 檢視，可能採用 ISO 高階結構（HSS）、加入氣候變遷條款。FDA QMSR 透過「by reference」整段引用此標準。

Global QMS standard for medical devices. Reconfirmed Mar 2025 with no changes; next review due 2026, possibly adopting ISO's harmonized structure (HSS) and climate clause. FDA QMSR incorporates this entirely by reference.

ISO 14971:2019

醫療器材風險管理。2025/3 再次確認、未修訂。核心：hazard 識別 → 風險估算 → 風險控制 → 殘餘風險 → benefit-risk → PMS 回饋。TR 24971:2020 為補充技術報告。

Risk management for medical devices. Reconfirmed Mar 2025, no revision. Core: hazard ID → risk analysis → control → residual risk → benefit-risk → PMS feedback. TR 24971:2020 is the supporting technical report.

IEC 62304 Edition 2（2026/9 發表）

醫療器材軟體生命週期。Ed.2 預定 2026/9 發表，重大變更：(1) 引入「process rigour levels」取代舊 Class A/B/C 安全分級——以開發程序的嚴謹度區分；(2) 新增 5.1.15 AI Plan 條款，要求 AI/ML 元件做專屬計畫；(3) 與 ISO 13485、14971、IEC 62366-1 整合更緊密。

Medical-device software lifecycle. Ed.2 publishes Sep 2026. Major changes: (1) introduces process rigour levels replacing the old A/B/C safety classes (now based on process rigor instead); (2) new clause 5.1.15 (AI Plan) requires a dedicated plan for AI/ML components; (3) tighter integration with ISO 13485, 14971, IEC 62366-1.

IEC 62366-1 + IEC 60601-1-6

人因 / 可用性工程。需完成 use specification、user interface specification、formative usability evaluation、summative usability evaluation；FDA 對 use-error 容忍度逐年下降。

Usability engineering. Must perform use specification, UI specification, formative + summative usability evaluations; FDA's tolerance for use-errors keeps tightening.

IEC 60601-1 + Collaterals

醫用電氣設備安全。-1-2 EMC（Ed. 4.1 2020）、-1-6 可用性、-1-8 警報、-1-9 環境設計、-1-10 PSP（生理閉迴路）、-1-11 家用環境、-1-12 緊急應變、-2-X 各裝置子標準。

Electrical safety for medical equipment. -1-2 EMC (Ed. 4.1 2020), -1-6 usability, -1-8 alarms, -1-9 eco design, -1-10 PSP (physiologic closed-loop), -1-11 home, -1-12 emergency, plus device-specific -2-X.

ISO 10993 Series

生物相容性。系列 18+ 部分覆蓋 cytotoxicity、sensitization、irritation、systemic toxicity、genotoxicity、implantation、hemocompatibility、chemical characterization、material identification。依「接觸時間 × 接觸部位」決定測試矩陣。

Biocompatibility. 18+ parts cover cytotoxicity, sensitization, irritation, systemic toxicity, genotoxicity, implantation, hemocompatibility, chemical characterization, material identification. Test matrix is selected by "contact duration × site."

臨床研究倫理

四、臨床研究倫理

現代臨床研究倫理建立於三份歷史文件之上：

  1. 紐倫堡公約 Nuremberg Code（1947）——回應納粹人體實驗，提出「自願知情同意是絕對必要」等十點原則。
  2. 赫爾辛基宣言 Declaration of Helsinki（1964，最新 2024 第 9 修訂）——世界醫學會（WMA）的醫學研究倫理原則，是全球臨床試驗的倫理北極星。
  3. 貝爾蒙特報告 Belmont Report（1979）——美國國家委員會回應 Tuskegee 梅毒研究醜聞，提出三大原則：尊重人 Respect for Persons、行善 Beneficence、正義 Justice，是 IRB 審查 framework 的根基。

實務上，每個涉及人類受試者的臨床研究都需經 IRB（Institutional Review Board）/ EC（Ethics Committee）事前審查；知情同意 informed consent 必含 21 CFR 50.25 列出的 8+5 必要元素（研究目的、程序、風險、利益、替代方案、保密、賠償、聯絡、撤回權……）。弱勢族群（vulnerable populations）——兒童、孕婦、囚犯、認知障礙者——需額外保護機制（21 CFR 50 Subparts B/C/D）。

Modern clinical-research ethics rests on three historical documents:

  1. Nuremberg Code (1947) — response to Nazi experimentation; "voluntary informed consent is absolutely essential" + 10 principles.
  2. Declaration of Helsinki (WMA, 1964; latest 2024 9th revision) — global north star for clinical-trial ethics.
  3. Belmont Report (1979) — US National Commission response to Tuskegee; three principles: Respect for Persons, Beneficence, Justice — the foundation of IRB review.

In practice, every human-subjects study needs prior IRB / Ethics Committee review; informed consent must contain the 8+5 elements per 21 CFR 50.25 (purpose, procedures, risks, benefits, alternatives, confidentiality, compensation, contacts, voluntary withdrawal…). Vulnerable populations — children, pregnant women, prisoners, cognitively impaired — require extra protections (21 CFR 50 Subparts B/C/D).

⚠️
利益衝突（COI）：研究者持股、顧問費、講課酬勞需揭露；許多期刊要求 ICMJE COI form。BME 工程師常同時為發明人 + 公司股東 + 臨床試驗 PI，COI 管理是學術倫理基本功。 Conflict of interest (COI): Researcher equity, consulting fees, speaker honoraria must be disclosed; many journals require an ICMJE COI form. BME engineers are often simultaneously inventor + shareholder + trial PI — managing COI is a core academic-ethics skill.
病人隱私

五、病人隱私

🇺🇸 HIPAA

1996 Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act。Privacy Rule (2003) 規範 PHI（受保護健康資訊）使用；Security Rule (2005) 規範電子 PHI 之 administrative / physical / technical safeguards。去識別化兩條路徑：Safe Harbor（移除 18 個 identifier）、Expert Determination（統計專家認證再識別風險極低）。違規最高罰 $1.5M/年 × 違規類別。

1996 Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. Privacy Rule (2003) governs use of PHI; Security Rule (2005) administrative/physical/technical safeguards for ePHI. De-identification: Safe Harbor (remove 18 identifiers) or Expert Determination (statistician certifies negligible re-id risk). Max penalty $1.5M/yr per category.

🇪🇺 GDPR

2016 通過、2018/5/25 生效。健康資料屬「特殊類別」需明示同意 / 立法授權；違規最高 €20M 或全球年營業額 4%（取高）。重要概念：data minimization、purpose limitation、right to erasure、data portability、DPO（Data Protection Officer）、跨境傳輸需 SCC / adequacy decision。

Passed 2016, effective 2018-05-25. Health data is "special category" needing explicit consent or legal basis; max penalty €20M or 4% global revenue. Key concepts: data minimization, purpose limitation, right to erasure, portability, DPO; cross-border transfers need SCCs or adequacy decision.

🇨🇦 PIPEDA

Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act（聯邦），健康資料許多由省級法管轄（Quebec Law 25、Ontario PHIPA）。要求合理 purpose、有限收集、明示同意（健康為敏感資料）。

Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (federal); health data largely under provincial law (Quebec Law 25, Ontario PHIPA). Requires reasonable purpose, limited collection, explicit consent (health data is sensitive).

🇹🇼 個人資料保護法（PDPA）

2012 修訂，醫療資料屬第 6 條特種資料，原則禁止蒐集除非具備同意 / 法令依據。2023/8 通過修正案成立個人資料保護委員會（2025 上路），與 GDPR 漸接軌。

2012 amended; medical data falls under Article 6 special category — collection generally prohibited absent consent or statutory basis. 2023-08 amendment established a Personal Data Protection Commission (live 2025), aligning toward GDPR.

💡
k-匿名性、l-多樣性、t-接近性：去識別化後的資料集中，每組 quasi-identifier 至少有 k 筆紀錄（k-anonymity）、每組敏感屬性至少有 l 種值（l-diversity）、敏感屬性分布與整體分布相近（t-closeness）。研究發布資料時的必修概念。 k-anonymity, l-diversity, t-closeness: A released dataset is k-anonymous if every quasi-identifier group has ≥k records; l-diverse if each group has ≥l distinct sensitive values; t-close if sensitive distributions resemble the global. Required reading before releasing research data.
AI 法規

六、AI 醫療專屬法規

  1. FDA GMLP（Good Machine Learning Practice，2021/10 共 10 條原則）——FDA + Health Canada + UK MHRA 共同發布。10 原則：多學科團隊、軟體工程最佳實務、代表性資料集、訓練/測試獨立、選擇參考標準、模型描述清晰、人在迴路、效能監測、清晰使用者溝通、上市後監測。
  2. PCCP（Predetermined Change Control Plan，2024/12 終版指南）——允許廠商預先描述未來模型更新範圍、流程、影響評估；同意後免再送 510(k)。
  3. EU AI Act（Regulation (EU) 2024/1689，2024/8/1 生效）——全球首部全面 AI 法。風險分四級：不可接受、高、有限、最小。醫療器材 AI 自動屬「高風險」（與 MDR/IVDR 重疊）。階段性執行：
    • 2025/2：禁止「不可接受風險」AI（操控行為、生物特徵分類）。
    • 2025/8：通用 AI 模型（GPAI）規範生效；training compute > 10²⁵ FLOPs 視為 systemic risk。
    • 2026/8：高風險 AI（含醫療）全面適用。
    • 2027/8：完整生效。
  4. TPLC（Total Product Life Cycle）——FDA 對 AI/ML SaMD 的整體治理思維：上市前 + 上市後資料 + 更新管理需協調為單一生命週期。
  1. FDA GMLP (Good Machine Learning Practice, Oct 2021, 10 principles) — co-published with Health Canada and UK MHRA. The 10 principles: multidisciplinary teams, software-engineering best practices, representative datasets, independent training/test, reference standards, clear model description, human-in-the-loop, performance monitoring, transparent user communication, post-market monitoring.
  2. PCCP (Predetermined Change Control Plan, final guidance Dec 2024) — sponsors describe future modification scope, process, and impact assessment up front; approved updates need no new 510(k).
  3. EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, in force 2024-08-01) — the world's first comprehensive AI law. Four risk tiers: unacceptable, high, limited, minimal. Medical-device AI is automatically "high-risk" (overlaps with MDR/IVDR). Phased enforcement:
    • Feb 2025: unacceptable-risk AI banned.
    • Aug 2025: general-purpose AI (GPAI) rules apply; training compute > 10²⁵ FLOPs = systemic risk.
    • Aug 2026: high-risk AI (incl. medical) fully applies.
    • Aug 2027: full applicability.
  4. TPLC (Total Product Life Cycle) — FDA's governance frame for AI/ML SaMD: premarket + postmarket data + update management as a single lifecycle.
召回管理

七、召回分級與案例

FDA 把醫療器材召回分三級：

  • Class I 召回——可能致死或不可逆重大傷害（最嚴重）。
  • Class II——可能造成暫時或可逆健康問題。
  • Class III——違反法規但不太可能造成傷害。

EU MDR 用「Field Safety Corrective Action (FSCA)」與「Field Safety Notice (FSN)」管理；廠商需主動通報主管機關 + 顧客 + 透過 Eudamed 公開。

FDA classifies recalls into three:

  • Class I recall: may cause death or serious injury (most severe).
  • Class II: may cause temporary or reversible health problems.
  • Class III: violations unlikely to cause harm.

EU MDR uses "Field Safety Corrective Action (FSCA)" + "Field Safety Notice (FSN)"; manufacturers must notify competent authorities + customers + Eudamed publicly.

🚨
個案：Philips Respironics CPAP（2021） — 史上最大規模 Class I 召回之一。2021/6 召回 1,700 萬+ 台 CPAP / BiPAP / 呼吸器含 PE-PUR 消音泡棉，泡棉可能降解釋出有毒微粒/化學物。Philips 後與美國司法部簽署 consent decree、賠償超過 11 億美元；Respironics 2024 暫停美國銷售。教訓：(1) 材料生物相容性（ISO 10993）+ 加速老化試驗應更嚴；(2) PMS 系統應更早偵測信號；(3) 顧客通報透明度。 Case: Philips Respironics CPAP (2021) — one of the largest Class I recalls ever. June 2021 recall of 17M+ CPAP/BiPAP/ventilators with PE-PUR foam that could degrade into toxic particles/chemicals. Philips signed a DOJ consent decree, paid >$1.1B settlement; Respironics paused US sales in 2024. Lessons: (1) tighter ISO 10993 biocompat + accelerated aging; (2) earlier PMS signal detection; (3) transparent customer notification.
決策樹

八、標準適用決策樹

🌳 標準適用決策樹

Q1.器材是否屬醫療器材？→ Yes：開始套用 ISO 13485 + ISO 14971 + 國家對應 GMP（QMSR / EU MDR）。Is it a medical device? → Yes: apply ISO 13485 + ISO 14971 + country GMP (QMSR / EU MDR).
Q2.是否含電氣或電池？→ 適用 IEC 60601-1 + 全套 collaterals（-1-2 EMC、-1-6 可用性、-1-8 警報…）+ 對應 -2-X 子標準。Does it have electricity or a battery? → Apply IEC 60601-1 + collaterals (-1-2 EMC, -1-6 usability, -1-8 alarms…) + -2-X subspecific.
Q3.是否含軟體？→ 適用 IEC 62304（2026/9 起為 Ed.2，含 AI Plan 條款）。Does it contain software? → Apply IEC 62304 (Ed.2 from Sep 2026, with AI Plan clause).
Q4.是否接觸人體（皮膚、黏膜、植入）？→ 套用 ISO 10993 系列（依接觸時間 × 部位選測試）。Does it contact the body (skin, mucosa, implant)? → Apply ISO 10993 (test matrix by duration × site).
Q5.是否含AI/ML 元件？→ 套用 FDA GMLP + 評估 PCCP；EU 上市另查 AI Act 高風險義務（2026/8 起）。Does it include AI/ML components? → Apply FDA GMLP + consider PCCP; for EU also EU AI Act high-risk obligations (from Aug 2026).
Q6.是否處理個人健康資料？→ 美國 HIPAA、歐盟 GDPR、台灣個資法；考慮 ISO 27001 / ISO 27799 資安控制。Does it handle personal health data? → US HIPAA, EU GDPR, Taiwan PDPA; consider ISO 27001 / ISO 27799 security controls.

程式示例

十、風險矩陣計算器

ISO 14971 的風險分析 = 估算每個 hazardous situation 的「嚴重度 severity × 發生頻率 probability」。下例以 5×5 矩陣回傳 risk class（acceptable / ALARP / unacceptable）。實務中「ALARP」(as low as reasonably practicable) 區的風險需文件化證明進一步降低不合理，否則仍須再控制。

ISO 14971 risk analysis = "severity × probability" of each hazardous situation. The example below returns a risk class (acceptable / ALARP / unacceptable) using a 5×5 matrix. ALARP-region risks must be documented as "further reduction is unreasonable"; otherwise additional control is required.

# ISO 14971-style 5x5 risk matrix
def risk_class(severity, probability):
    """
    severity:    1=Negligible, 2=Minor, 3=Serious, 4=Critical, 5=Catastrophic
    probability: 1=Improbable, 2=Remote, 3=Occasional, 4=Probable, 5=Frequent
    Returns: 'Acceptable' | 'ALARP' | 'Unacceptable'
    """
    score = severity * probability
    if score <= 4:    return "Acceptable"
    elif score <= 12:  return "ALARP"
    else:              return "Unacceptable"

hazards = [
    ("Electrical shock",    5, 2),    # Catastrophic, Remote
    ("Software false-neg",  4, 3),    # Critical, Occasional
    ("Skin irritation",     2, 4),    # Minor, Probable
]
for name, s, p in hazards:
    print(f"{name:25s}  S={s}  P={p}  → {risk_class(s,p)}")
% ISO 14971-style 5x5 risk matrix
function rc = risk_class(severity, probability)
    score = severity * probability;
    if     score <= 4,  rc = 'Acceptable';
    elseif score <= 12, rc = 'ALARP';
    else,             rc = 'Unacceptable';
    end
end

hazards = {'Electrical shock',   5, 2; ...
           'Software false-neg', 4, 3; ...
           'Skin irritation',    2, 4};
for k = 1:size(hazards,1)
    name = hazards{k,1}; s = hazards{k,2}; p = hazards{k,3};
    fprintf('%-22s  S=%d  P=%d  -> %s\n', name, s, p, risk_class(s,p));
end

結語

十二、結語

恭喜你走到全本生醫工程概論的最後一章。這 20 章覆蓋了從解剖生理、訊號與影像、力學與材料、裝置與感測到神經、細胞基因、AI、法規倫理的全圖。但 BME 真正的學習才剛開始——這個領域以「每三年翻新一次」的速度演進：新療法（CRISPR、CAR-T、基因編輯體內遞送）、新模態（柔性腦機介面、Apple Vision Pro 整合 AR 手術）、新法規（QMSR、IEC 62304 Ed.2、EU AI Act 階段執行）、新工具（醫療 LLM、MCPmed、基礎模型）——你會發現本章的時間戳幾年後就過時。

但有些東西不會過時：把工程做到對病人負責、做到對法規透明、做到對未來可重現。當你設計一個植入物，請假設它要陪一個人活 30 年；當你訓練一個診斷模型，請假設它會被部署在你最不熟悉的人口；當你寫一份 DHF，請假設未來會有檢視員、稽核員、自己的下一代工程師看到它。BME 工程不是寫程式 / 做硬體 / 跑試驗——是用工程語言對「健康」這件事負責。

下一步：把這本教材作為地圖，跟你最感興趣的章節深入；找一個臨床合作者，跟著他們去醫院看一台真正的器材如何被使用；讀一篇 PMA summary、一份 510(k) decision letter、一份 EU MDR Notified Body audit report。當你看懂這些「現實文件」，你就真的進入了 BME。

祝你長路順利。

The timestamps in this chapter will go stale in a few years.

What won't go stale: doing engineering that is accountable to patients, transparent to regulators, and reproducible for the future. When you design an implant, assume it must accompany someone for 30 years. When you train a diagnostic model, assume it will be deployed in the populations you know least. When you write a DHF, assume future reviewers, auditors, and the next generation of engineers will read it. BME isn't coding, hardware, or running trials — it's using engineering language to take responsibility for "health."

Next step: treat this textbook as a map, dive deeply into the chapters that excite you, find a clinical collaborator and watch a real device get used in a hospital, read a PMA summary, a 510(k) decision letter, and an EU MDR Notified Body audit report. When you can parse these "real documents," you've truly entered BME.

Wishing you the long road ahead.
